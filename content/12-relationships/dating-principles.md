# Dating & Relationship Principles

## Core Principles

**If he wanted to, he really would.** When someone wants you: they make time, initiate, are consistent, are clear, show up. When they don't: "busy," "not ready," "figuring things out," "dealing with a lot." Stop making excuses for people who don't choose you. Confusion about where you stand = your answer.

**Men are supposed to fall in love with you falling in love with yourself.**

**Indecision is a red flag.** Difference between "not sure" and scared. Fear can come from a trigger; simple indecisiveness is a no. If someone isn't FULLY committed to you, it's one hell of a no. Don't let someone's "idk" stop you from being someone's "HELL YES."

**Date like a man** — your goals are your priority. Mission comes first. Dating fits into your life, doesn't consume it. You're selective about who gets your time. Don't cancel plans with friends for a date. Don't pause your job search for someone new.

**Stop talking about men.** Over-analyzing gives them energy they haven't earned. Test: if you removed all conversation about men from your life, what would you talk about? Focus on that.

**Bring back silliness and flirtiness.** Modern dating is too serious too soon. Chemistry needs play. Attraction needs lightness. Suggest fun activities, not just dinner. Use humor. Be present.

## The Free Pass Rule
Everyone gets 1–2 early on for genuine mistakes, miscommunications, bad days. Not for disrespect, dishonesty, patterns, or major violations.
- First time: "Interesting, I'll watch for patterns"
- Second time: "Okay, this might be who they are"
- Third time: "This IS who they are — time to decide"

## What You Want

Physical: hot, tall, big shoulders, abs. High sex drive, sexually adventurous. Hedge fund guy, actor, musician, hot athlete energy.

## Desperation Signals to Cut
- Canceling plans with friends for a date
- Pausing your job search for someone new
- Over-analyzing every text with friends
- Applying to men the same way you'd apply to a job out of desperation

## Boundaries List
- Don't sleep over at men's houses until in an established relationship. Maintains mystery, protects energy, keeps you in control, forces them to plan better dates.
- With sex: let them pleasure you first. Sets the tone; ensures you're not an afterthought.
- First red flag: notice it. Second: address it directly. Third: exit. Don't wait for it to get better.
- Don't commit until YOU decide they've earned it — not because you're tired of being alone, not because it's "time."

## The Placeholder Man
Don't let someone's "idk" stop you from being someone's "HELL YES." If someone isn't fully committed to you, that's a no. Indecision = a placeholder relationship. Exit and free yourself for a certain yes.

## Power Dynamics
When two people are competing for you — don't choose too quickly; let them both show you who they are. When you're competing with someone else — you've already lost. The right person chooses you clearly.

## Ending Things
- Soft exit: "Do you wanna hang out sometime?" — then don't follow up.
- Direct: "I don't think we're a match, but I wish you well."
- For mismatch: "You're not what I expected you to be like." True, not cruel, final.

## Live Your Life Like You'll Never Get Married
Build a life so full that partnership is optional: career that fulfills, friendships that sustain, financial independence, a home you love. (See [[4C-reflections]])

## Hard Truths

- Guys can end up in a relationship with you because "you really like them" — avoid this by being a massive cunt to them so they're only with you because they genuinely love you
- When you're hooking up with someone who doesn't want to be with you, you have to live with the realisation that they think you are embarrassing and that they could do better
- You don't need roughness when you're actually attracted to someone
- When you lose their respect there's nothing you can do to get it back
- In most relationships they will notice your shortcomings and fall out of love

## Sex and Power

- What makes sex sexy is that it's an open-ended negotiation of a power relationship that hasn't yet been fully decided about where exactly things will settle. That's also why it's difficult to maintain excitement in long-term relationships where the settling has already occurred.
- In a long-term relationship that maintains the excitement of good sex, you need to make sure that you are both constantly becoming more powerful. You need to be catalysing the power increase of your partner so there remains ever more to negotiate.
