# Creative Ideas

- *Late to Life* — protagonist only became ensouled at 27. Always a late bloomer. A story about late-arriving self-awareness.

**Substack/Essays:**
- *My Years of Convalescence* — personal essay on recovery and healing.

- Write about Gen Suleimani.

## Story & Novel Concepts

- **Alien comes into human girl's body and dates in NYC** — shocked by the cruelty of it. What does kindness mean when everyone else is brutal?
- **Asian daughter gets everyone who loves her to hate her, so when she dies no one is hurt.** (Preemptive sacrifice.)
- **Bored teenage girl posts online as a joke, accidentally gets elected dictator with Kim Jong-il numbers.** (Consent and power.)
- **Frankenstein's monster story—me trying to fix my mind,** projecting it into a monstrous creation that can astroturf after continuous psychiatric and therapeutic interventions. (Self-reconstruction.)
- **Someone has an AI-generated kid. Kid doesn't know she's AI. Others don't either.**
- **She gets a cure to erase herself from world memory—as a way to kill herself without causing pain.**
- **Surveillance state via housewives' neighbourhood watch.** (Domestic surveillance, trust, betrayal.)
- **What if we used VC money to program God.** (AI, spirituality, hubris.)
