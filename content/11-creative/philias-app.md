# Philias — Friendship Tracking App

A friendship OS. Tracks the people you love, the quality of your connections, and the provenance of how friendships formed.

Live link: https://philias.lovable.app

---

## Design Feedback

- Ocean bubbles: softer inner shimmer, softer edge, subtle sparkle particle that floats off close friends occasionally
- The dossier feels like a settings panel — should feel like a portrait. Add: journal-prompt section headers, faint watercolor gradient per person using their color, softer label typography
- The Garden: different card backgrounds per ghost, soft illustrated texture, cards slightly tilted like scattered photos

### Features to Add
- "Last thing they said that stayed with me" — one line, replaces itself when something new takes over
- "Who introduced us" — provenance of a friendship is meaningful and nobody tracks it
- Seasonal check-in nudge — soft prompt when you open the app: "it's been a while since you updated Ana. still true?"
- "What I'd eulogy them with" — buried deep in the dossier, optional, clarifying, not morbid

## Room System Concept (Alternative Architecture)

Each person gets their own world with these rooms:

- **Scenes** — private stream: "I can't stop thinking about," "I noticed," "everything shifted when"
- **The Altar** — images only. Receipts, screenshots, a flower, a drawing, a meme. No captions required.
- **Their Universe** — zodiac, favorites (song, food, color, era, scent), cravings
- **Rituals** — named things you do together: "The walk." "How we always order." "The way we say goodbye."
- **Signs** — serendipity log: things in the world that felt like them showing up
- **Unsaid** — one field. Things you can't say out loud to anyone but this page.
- **Who brought you together** — provenance
- **Theory** — mythology of this specific friendship

Additional fields: "last thing they said that stayed" (replaces itself), "era" tag (what era of your life + are you still in it)

## Friendship Tree Concept

Provenance is the most under-recorded fact about any friendship. The tree has roots in people who first put you in the same room with someone, and grows new branches every time a friend introduces you to another. Aristotle's philia-as-shared-activity becomes literal: friendship reproduces by friction.
