# 1000 Lives — Living Wide, Not Just Long

The goal: live 1000 lives by the time you die.

Not one long linear life with a single identity. A life made of many threads — careers, obsessions, cities, personas, skills, eras. The person who by 80 has been a researcher, a writer, an athlete, a traveler, someone who knew obscure things deeply and surprised people constantly.

## What This Means in Practice

**Thread maxxing** — keep multiple live threads going simultaneously. A career thread, a creative thread, a learning thread, a body thread, a relationship thread. None of these cancels the others. The goal is parallel aliveness, not serial focus.

**Side quests are not distractions** — they are the content of the 1000 lives. The Brazilian jiu-jitsu phase, the French lessons, the period of becoming obsessed with Islamic history, the month spent understanding SMRs — these don't dilute the main story. They are the main story.

**Avatars** — each version of you is an avatar, not the final form. You can shed them without grief. The version who was obsessed with debate, the version in Barcelona, the version applying to climate VC — all real, all valid, all part of the total count.

## The Risk to Avoid

Breadth without depth becomes trivia. Each thread needs enough commitment to produce real knowledge, real skill, real experience — not just surface contact. A life of 1000 shallow dips isn't 1000 lives; it's one restless life.

The standard: go deep enough in each thread that you changed because of it.
