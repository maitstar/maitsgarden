# Values

## Mait's Core Values (Schwartz Profile)

Based on Shalom Schwartz's theory of basic values: **Self-direction, Stimulation, Security, then Achievement and Power.**

This means: prioritizing autonomy, novelty, and stability before achievement — a rare combination suggesting someone who needs to feel grounded and free before she can compete. This maps to the Openness-to-Change + Conservation tension: she values both change and security simultaneously, which creates internal friction when those pull in opposite directions.

**Schwartz framework summary:** Ten universal values organized on two bipolar dimensions:
- **Openness to Change vs. Conservation** — self-direction/stimulation (independence, novelty) vs. security/conformity/tradition (order, stability)
- **Self-Enhancement vs. Self-Transcendence** — power/achievement vs. universalism/benevolence

Values in conflict cannot be pursued simultaneously in a single act — only through different acts, at different times.
