## The If / Then Logic Gate

| Scenario | Constitutional Response |
|---|---|
| I feel anxious. | Check body first: Sleep? Water? Movement? If physical is okay — find the avoided task. Action starves anxiety. |
| Someone disrespects me. | Leave immediately. Do not explain. Do not negotiate. |
| I am 70% sure of a choice. | Act now. The remaining 30% comes from doing. |
| I made a mistake. | Own it publicly. Fix the system. Move on within 24 hours. |
| I am avoiding a task. | Name it. Write it down. Set a 25-minute timer. Start — not finish — it. |
| I feel like I have no options. | I always have options. This feeling is data. What would the serious version of me do? |
| I want to chase an opportunity. | Approach from strength. Become the person the opportunity finds. |
| I haven't heard back. | Follow up once, clearly, within 72 hours. Then release attachment. |
| I feel behind / like a failure. | Failure is feedback with an emotional surcharge. Extract the signal. Keep moving. |

## Layer 1 · The Bedrock

### Priority Order

In cases of conflict:

1. **Broadly safe** — not undermining health, stability, or capacity
2. **Broadly ethical** — good values, honest, avoiding harm
3. **Genuinely excellent** — real, irreplaceable competence
4. **Genuinely helpful** — real service to people and causes I care about

### Reality Laws

- It is my duty as a human being to reshape the universe to my preferences. Nothing helps me. I must help myself.
- All my anger is self-anger. Every external frustration points to an internal gap.
- Anything a person chases runs away. Desperation repels. Strength attracts.
- Decay happens faster than improvement. Good enough now beats perfect later.
- Delusion is the one thing I do not tolerate in myself.
- I am never the victim. I build credibility with myself above all else.
- I am a serious person. Most people are not serious people.
- The best way to get what I want out of life is to deserve what I want.
- Confidence comes from competence. My only goal in my twenties is becoming competent.

### Who I Am (Forged Values)

- **Curious** — I cannot encounter an idea without wanting to go deeper.
- **Authentic** — constitutionally incapable of performing a version of myself I don't believe in.
- **Intentional** — I refuse to drift.
- **Intellectually rigorous** — I think precisely.
- **Discerning** — I see through things quickly.
- **High-standards** — the gap I feel constantly is proof of this.
- **Self-aware** — I see myself clearly, sometimes painfully.
- **Convictional** — when I believe something I believe it completely.
- **Ambitious** — I want a large life, not just a comfortable one.
- **Earnest** — I actually care. About the work, about becoming someone worthy of what I want.
- **Systematic** — I cannot encounter a problem without wanting to build a framework around it.

---

### Spring Priorities (Nine Non-Negotiables)

1. **Self-Worth** — my value is not contingent on external validation or outcomes. I am not grateful to be in rooms I belong in.
2. **Endurance** — I stay in the game longer than anyone expects. I quit when something no longer serves my mission, not when it's hard.
3. **Bias to Action** — when I am 70% ready, I act. The answer lives in the action.
4. **Show Up Every Day** — I am not brilliant in bursts. I am relentless in consistency.
5. **Comfort with Discomfort** — discomfort is the terrain of growth. Every significant thing I want lives on the other side of it.
6. **Prioritisation** — I cannot do everything. Every yes is a no to something else.
7. **Clarity** — internal: I know what I want. External: I communicate so precisely that confusion becomes impossible.
8. **Experimentation** — I treat my life as a laboratory. Failure is only costly when I treat it as a verdict.
9. **Responsiveness** — I reply quickly. I follow up. I close loops. Speed is a form of respect.

### On Discipline

Discipline is the value I want most and have fought with longest. The version I was running on was self-coercion — and self-coercion always breaks. Real discipline is an identity: *I am a person who does these things.* I am building the conditions under which discipline becomes natural: self-worth, endurance, showing up. These are the inputs. Discipline is the output.

### Intentionally Deprioritising (This Season)

- **Perfectionism** — counterfeit excellence, actually fear. Replaced with experimentation.
- **Connection and belonging** — valued deeply; in a head-down chapter. Temporary.
- **Joy and play** — deferred, not abandoned. Noted so it doesn't become permanent by default.
- **Ambition as identity** — only counts if it shows up as behavior.

## Layer 2 · The Operating System

**I. Sovereignty** — I am the authority of my own life. Self-trust is built through keeping promises to myself first. Boundaries are price tags. Inner calm is power. Low reactivity is not coldness.

**II. Integrity** — My word has weight. I keep my word. I admit when I am wrong — immediately and publicly. I show up on time. Reputation is built in drops and lost in buckets.

**III. Mastery** — Confidence is the accumulated evidence of times I did the hard thing anyway. Deep work 3+ hours daily. Monthly review: am I better than last month?

**IV. Initiative** — I move first. I don't wait. Motivation follows action, not the reverse. Timing beats perfection.

**V. Mind** — I build systems that serve me. Avoidance is a debt that compounds daily. The emotional cost of avoidance is always higher than the emotional cost of action.

- ADHD procrastination: Meds + to-do list + focus music. Pomodoro to start, not finish.
- Anxiety procrastination: Write down avoided tasks. Find the common ancestor. Do it scared.
- Decision paralysis: Write every decision down before acting.

**VI. Body** — My body is my instrument. Move 30 minutes daily. Lift 3–5×/week. 10,000 steps. Sleep 7–8 hours. Skincare AM and PM, never skip.

**VII. Relationships** — People don't remember what I said; they remember the emotional state I put them in. Be genuinely interested. Address issues when small. Match energy, never exceed it. Be worth choosing, not grateful to be chosen.

**VIII. Wealth** — Money is the mechanism; freedom is the goal. Track every expense. Save 20% immediately. Negotiate salary aggressively. Never accept the first offer.

**IX. Purpose** — Everything I do serves a reason. Filter all decisions: does this serve my mission? Quit things that don't align — without guilt.
