# 🧱 Foundation

I am emotionally regulated and resilient. My depression is managed through practices I actually do, support I actually use, and a relationship with myself I actually tend.

I know who I am. I know what I value. I know what I will and will not accept. I trust my own judgment even when the outcome is unclear.

I work with my ADD brain and not against it. Focus comes when I create the conditions for it. I rest without guilt. I am someone who recovers, not someone who avoids.

---

# 💼 Work

I am excellent at what I do — not in a generalist way, but in a way that is hard to replicate. What I do has changed shape more than once on the way to where I'm going. I take the best available path and go all the way.

I am in a role that genuinely excites me — intellectually alive, well compensated, with real stakes and real feedback. They wanted me specifically. I am chosen.

I show up with genuine credibility, genuine curiosity, and seriousness. People want to work with me. People want to hire me.

I work on something stimulating that compensates me well and puts me in a room with people operating at high levels. I trust that if I keep following what genuinely interests me and go deep, I will land somewhere I couldn't have planned.

I write. I speak. I contribute beyond my immediate role. I speak at conferences and panels because I have built a point of view worth hearing. The answers always find me.

I am building toward running something of my own. Every role is a rehearsal for that. My work creates real impact.

I work at the intersection of capital, systems, and the future — the place where money meets consequence, where a well-framed argument can redirect millions. It doesn't matter exactly what it's called. What matters is that I am thinking about something real, something that has stakes, and I am the person in the room who understands it more completely than anyone expected.

A great day: I read something in the morning that changes how I see something I thought I understood. I write a paragraph that didn't exist before. I am in a conversation where I have to be completely present. By the end, something has moved.

The thing I am building toward is something of my own — a firm, a fund, an institution, a body of work. I don't know its exact shape yet and I have stopped needing to. I know what it does: it takes something complicated and makes it legible. It moves resources toward things that matter. It has my name on it because I built it, not because I was handed it.

# 💰 Money

I am financially literate. I understand my money. I make informed decisions. I am building wealth deliberately.

Money is not a barrier to travel, experiences, or opportunities. I have multiple income streams. I am financially secure and abundant — I never have to worry about money.

I have a full, robust emergency fund and investments. I can afford the luxuries I like without fearing debt. I have no debt. I live without external constraints. I am financially free.

I have a home that feels like mine — beautiful, calm, intentional.

The debt is gone. I remember the day I closed the last account — not with fanfare, quietly, on a Tuesday. It felt like putting down something I had been carrying so long I forgot the weight was optional.

Financial freedom feels like this: I see a flight and I book it without doing the math. I order what I actually want. I say yes to experiences without calculating whether I can afford it afterward. The answer is always yes. It has been yes for a while now, and I have gotten used to it without losing gratitude for it.

My investments are working while I sleep. I understand where my money is and why. I made these decisions deliberately, with knowledge, not anxiety. I have built something that builds itself.

# 🌿 Body

I feel good in my body. I am strong, healthy, and at ease in my own skin. I move well. I have energy — a lift, a walk, a stretch. My body is capable and it shows. I eat intuitively, without guilt. I have no deficiencies.

I sleep by 11 and wake before 8 consistently. I sleep really, really well.

I am beautiful — even when the world is not immediately telling me so. I carry myself like someone dropped at gorgeous. I am well groomed, well dressed, and put together without it being effortful or anxious. My skin is clear. My hair is healthy. My style is intentional. I look like myself, fully realized. People find me beautiful. Some of them say so. I receive it gracefully, and it does not move the floor of my self-worth either way — because my floor is solid.

I have a lean, beautiful body. A tiny waist, feminine abs, smooth skin, thick hair. Dropped at gorgeous.

My morning is mine and it is not effortful. I wake before 8. I do something with my body — stretch, lift, walk — not because I should but because I feel better when I do, and I have been doing it long enough to know that. My skincare takes ten minutes and works. My hair is healthy and I know how to take care of it.

My style is intentional without being precious. I know what I look good in and I own it. I am not chasing trends. I am recognizably myself in how I dress — there is a coherence, an aesthetic, a signal. When I walk into a room I look like I know who I am.

Movement is joy, not punishment. I move because my body likes it. I am strong in ways that show and in ways that don't.

# 🌍 Life

I live in New York. I have legal status. I have a home that is clean, organized, sunny, mine — a place I am glad to return to after a day.

I have a tight friend group I am consistently in touch with. I also have a wide, warm network of weak ties — people across industries, cities, and worlds who think of me when something interesting comes up. Serendipity finds me. I am included. I am an insider in multiple rooms. This happened because I showed up, stayed curious, and was genuinely interested in people.

I travel regularly — to beautiful places, to places that expand me, to places that change how I see. Money is not a variable. Time and desire are the only variables.

I have hobbies I do for pure joy. My life has side quests. It stays playful. I don't let it become too serious. I celebrate my life in beautiful places with people I love — for reasons both large and small. I don't wait for others to organize the moment.

# 🕯️ Spirituality

I am connected to something larger than my ambitions. I don't have a name for it that satisfies me, and I have stopped needing one.

I have a practice — not perfectly kept, but real. Something that returns me to my body when I have been living too much in my head. Yoga. Ayurveda. Moments of actual silence. I treat my body as a system I want to understand, not a problem I want to fix. I am curious about ancient knowledge — the kind that doesn't need a clinical trial to be true.

I go on pilgrimages of a kind. To places that ask something of me. To practitioners who know things I don't. I take these seriously because they have taught me things about myself that no amount of analysis could have surfaced.

I have a relationship with my own intuition. I listen to it. I have learned to tell the difference between fear and knowing. I trust the latter.

# 💫 Love

I am with someone exceptional. Not as a status symbol — but because I am genuinely attracted to people who have built something, who take their life seriously, who operate with integrity and intelligence.

He is distractingly handsome, successful, and secure. He chose me publicly and proudly. He has never been in love like this and he doesn't hide it. He is devoted. I arrived at this relationship whole. His choosing me is a celebration, not a confirmation of my worth. This makes me available to be loved.

Our relationship is high energy, passionate, adventurous, and happy. We bring out the best in each other. I am attracted to him in ways that do not fade. It feels like a crash for both of us — a little electric, a little unsteady, even when things are ordinary. He makes me feel desired in a way that is specific to me. He wants me as his first choice.

The physical is alive. There is passion, desire, real chemistry that makes everything vivid.

He never had to be desperate, and it shows — in how he carries himself, how he makes decisions, how he treats people who can do nothing for him. He operates from abundance, not hunger. His psychological freedom is palpable.

He is established enough that I will never have to be the foundation alone, and secure enough that my success doesn't threaten him. He chose me from a place of complete freedom — nothing clouding it.

He takes care of his body. Lean, disciplined, physically alive. He holds himself to high standards. Attraction is real. He thinks I am dropped at gorgeous.

He is a good person. He shows up for people around him without needing credit. He is generous without an audience. He is principled. He is a man — people notice. He is present.

He moves through the world at a level that fills rooms. Being with him means belonging to a world I want access to — not as a passenger, but as someone who belongs there in her own right.

He is tall. He physically takes up space in the right way. He has a quality where he could have had anyone, and something about him makes it obvious — and he chose me, and doesn't make me feel uncertain about it. He is charismatic. He doesn't perform up or down for any room.

He is calm and easygoing in a way only genuinely secure people are. Fun. Flirty in how he moves through the world. Young at heart and mature in the ways that matter. Devoted. Love at first sight. I admire him. He teaches me things because he wants to bring me into what he knows.

# 🧵 Thread

I become someone whose inner life is as developed as her outer ambition.

I am continuously evolving. I will never be finished, and I have made peace with that. I will never peak, and I have made peace with that. I am constantly improving.

I have clarity about my purpose and I pursue it with conviction. I keep my word to myself. This makes everything possible.

I write and I publish. Not just reports and memos — I write stories. Narrative. The kind of writing where you put a real human being in the middle of something true and let them move through it. I am working on a book. It has changed shape more than once because I have changed shape more than once, and that is fine. What doesn't change is that I will finish it. I will publish it. People will read it and something in them will shift.

I write shorter things too — essays, pieces, dispatches. I publish them. They find readers. Some of those readers write back.

My creative practice is daily and partly private — not everything is for an audience. I write things no one will read. I make things without a purpose. This is how I think. This is how I stay in contact with myself.

My work creates meaningful impact. I am genuinely, undeniably smart, and I use it — not to perform intelligence but to go deep, to understand, to contribute something real. My intelligence has always been one of my most reliable things. I stopped hiding it a long time ago. I stopped apologizing for it even longer ago than that.

I have a village. A circle of people who actually know me, show up for me, help me. I do not feel alone in the way I have felt alone. I am loved by people I have chosen and who have chosen me back.
