#journalling 


Reflecting on the previous quarter
Q1: What did I accomplish in the past three months? Q2: What helped my achieve these results? Q3: What else went well? Q4: What did I start but not finish? Q5: What did I not start that I intended to? Q6: What else happened that didn’t go well? Q7: Overall, how do I feel about the past three months?
Planning for the next quarter
Q1: How do I want to feel over the next three months? Q2: What do I want to start doing / do more of / do less of / stop doing? Q3: What is ONE THING I can accomplish in the next three months that would impact my life in a meaningful way? Q4: What are some concrete steps I can take to achieve this? Q5: What other goals will I accomplish the next three months? Q6: Overall how am I feeling about the next quarter? Q7: What beliefs or values that I hold will most assist me in achieving my goals for the next quarter? Q8: What are three words that describe my next quarter?

**Why 5 years?**
- Long enough to accomplish major shit (get promoted, move countries, build real skills)
- Short enough to feel real and urgent (not "someday" but "by 2030")
- Far enough to be ambitious, close enough to be accountable
**The Structure:** Five categories, 3-10 goals each. Each goal should be:
- **Specific:** "Get promoted to VP" not "advance career"
- **Measurable:** You'll know if you did it or not
- **Dated:** Even if roughly (by 2027, by end of 5 years, etc.)
- **Actionable:** You can take steps toward it starting now

## **CATEGORY 1: PERSONAL**
These are goals about YOU as an individual—your body, your mind, your skills, your habits, your self-development.
## **Physical Health & Fitness**
**Core prompts:**
- What do you want your body to be able to do in 5 years?
- What fitness baseline do you want to maintain?
- What physical goal would make you proud?
- What health issues do you want resolved or managed?
**Specific questions:**
- Strength: What can you lift? How many pull-ups? What bodyweight exercises?
- Endurance: Run how far? Bike how far? Swim how far?
- Body composition: What weight? What body fat %? What visual goal?
- Flexibility/mobility: What movements? What yoga poses? What range of motion?
- Consistency: Exercise how many times per week for 5 years?
- Sports/activities: What can you do competently? What skill level?
- Health metrics: Blood pressure, cholesterol, resting heart rate targets?
- Energy: How do you want to feel day-to-day?
**Examples:**
- Run a half marathon by 2027
- Do 10 unassisted pull-ups by 2028
- Maintain 18% body fat
- Exercise 5x/week consistently for 5 years
- Deadlift 200 lbs by 2029
- Complete a sprint triathlon
- Get to advanced level in yoga (specific poses)
- Have stable energy without afternoon crashes
- Eliminate chronic \[specific health issue\]

## **Mental Health & Executive Function**
**Core prompts:**
- What do you want figured out about your brain by 2030?
- What mental health baseline do you want?
- What executive function improvements matter most?
- What support systems or treatments do you want in place?
**Specific questions:**
- Diagnosis: Complete assessment for ADHD? Other conditions?
- Treatment: Find medication that works? Therapy type? Frequency?
- Executive function: What strategies for task initiation? Organization? Follow-through?
- Habits: What daily/weekly practices? What consistency?
- Phone/tech: What relationship with phone? Screen time goals?
- Stress management: What coping mechanisms work? Use them how often?
- Baseline: What does "managing well" look like day-to-day?
**Examples:**
- Complete ADHD diagnosis and find effective medication by 2026
- Reduce daily phone screen time to under 3 hours
- Maintain weekly therapy for 5 years
- Build task initiation systems that actually work
- Manage anxiety without it derailing productivity
- Have 3+ coping strategies for stress that you actually use
- Maintain meditation practice 5x/week
- Function well enough to meet work deadlines consistently
- Build systems for executive function that don't require constant willpower

## **Skills & Learning (Personal)**
**Core prompts:**
- What do you want to be able to do in 5 years that you can't do now?
- What skills would make you feel more complete?
- What have you always wanted to learn?
- What would you learn if it had nothing to do with career?
**Specific questions:**
- Languages: Which language(s)? To what level? By when?
- Creative: What artistic/creative skills? To what proficiency?
