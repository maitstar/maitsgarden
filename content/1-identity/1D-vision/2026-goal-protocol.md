## **Evidence-Based Sequential Framework**

## **PHASE 1: BASELINE ASSESSMENT**
**Timeline:** Days 1-3 (Late December) 
**Objective:** Establish accurate baseline before intervention
## **Protocol 1.1: Year Retrospective (Day 1)**
**Time Required:** 60-90 minutes **Materials:** Pen, paper (hand-writing required for neural encoding)
**Execute in sequence:**
1. List all 2025 wins and highlights
2. Identify wins in disguise (failures that produced learning)
3. Document what worked vs. what didn't
4. Compare where you showed up for yourself vs. abandoned yourself
5. Identify recurring patterns
6. What did I know and still choose poorly 
7. what am I not willing to roleratw
8. List what you're proud of
9. Document what you learned
10. Favorite new thing tried
11. Best thing bought (emphasizing self-investment)
12. Synthesize 2025 in one word or sentence
## **Protocol 1.2: Life Wheel Assessment (Day 1)**
**Time Required:** 15 minutes **Method:** Rate 1-10 scale with defined anchors
**Execute:**
1. Define what each number (1-10) means to you before rating
2. Rate these 8 domains:
	- Personal Growth
	- Health
	- Love/Self-Love
	- Relationships
	- Leisure
	- Spirituality
	- Career
	- Finance
1. Plot on circular diagram
1. Identify lowest-scoring domains (these are blind spots)
## **Protocol 1.3: Accomplishments Inventory (Day 2)**
**Time Required:** 45 minutes **Rationale:** Counter negative bias in memory (Balcetis research: memory of progress is faulty)
**Create two lists:**
**List A: Planned & Accomplished**
- Things you set out to do and completed
- Include both large and small
**List B: Unexpected Victories**
- Things accomplished that weren't planned
- Surprise wins
**Critical:** This becomes monthly practice. Your brain has negativity bias—objective data corrects this.
## **Protocol 1.4: Energy/Drain Analysis (Day 2)**
**Time Required:** 30 minutes
**Create two lists:**
**——**
**List A: What Made You Happy**
- Specific moments/activities that brought joy
- Use for decision-making in 2026
**List B: More Of / Less Of**
- More of: What brought joy, energy, growth
- Less of: What drained, didn't serve you
## **Protocol 1.5: Blind Spot Identification (Day 3)**
**Time Required:** 45 minutes **Method:** Multi-source triangulation
**Execute:**
1. Review Life Wheel (lowest scoring = blind spots)
2. Request feedback from 3-5 trusted people
3. Review 2025 struggles for patterns
4. Identify avoidance patterns
5. Document explicitly
6. For each blind spot, design mitigation protocol
**Critical:** Unacknowledged blind spots = planning failure. Name them explicitly.
## **Protocol 1.6: Transition Protocol (Day 3)**
**Time Required:** 30 minutes
**Answer:**
- What crosses into 2026?
- What stays in 2025?
**Optional:** Write closure letters (don't send) to people or former selves.
