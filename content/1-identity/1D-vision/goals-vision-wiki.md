# Goals and Vision

## My Vision: The Manifesto

**Foundation:**
I am emotionally regulated and resilient. My depression is managed through practices I actually do, support I actually use, and a relationship with myself I actually tend. I know who I am. I know what I value. I know what I will and will not accept. I trust my own judgment even when the outcome is unclear. I work with my ADD brain and not against it. Focus comes when I create the conditions for it. I rest without guilt. I am someone who recovers, not someone who avoids.

**Work:**
I am excellent at what I do — not in a generalist way, but in a way that is hard to replicate. What I do has changed shape more than once on the way to where I'm going. I take the best available path and go all the way. I am in a role that genuinely excites me — intellectually alive, well compensated, with real stakes and real feedback. They wanted me specifically. I am chosen. I work at the intersection of capital, systems, and the future — the place where money meets consequence, where a well-framed argument can redirect millions.

A great day: I read something in the morning that changes how I see something I thought I understood. I write a paragraph that didn't exist before. I am in a conversation where I have to be completely present. By the end, something has moved. The thing I am building toward is something of my own — a firm, a fund, an institution, a body of work. It takes something complicated and makes it legible. It moves resources toward things that matter. It has my name on it because I built it.

**Money:**
I am financially literate. I understand my money. I make informed decisions. I am building wealth deliberately. Money is not a barrier to travel, experiences, or opportunities. I have multiple income streams. I am financially secure and abundant. Financial freedom feels like this: I see a flight and I book it without doing the math. I order what I actually want. My investments are working while I sleep.

**Body:**
I feel good in my body. I am strong, healthy, and at ease in my own skin. I sleep by 11 and wake before 8 consistently. I am beautiful — even when the world is not immediately telling me so. I carry myself like someone dropped at gorgeous. My skin is clear. My hair is healthy. My style is intentional. I look like myself, fully realized. I have a lean, beautiful body. A tiny waist, feminine abs, smooth skin, thick hair. Dropped at gorgeous. Movement is joy, not punishment. I move because my body likes it.

**Life:**
I live in New York. I have legal status. I have a home that is clean, organized, sunny, mine. I have a tight friend group I am consistently in touch with. I also have a wide, warm network of weak ties — people across industries, cities, and worlds who think of me when something interesting comes up. I travel regularly — to beautiful places, to places that expand me. Money is not a variable. Time and desire are the only variables. I have hobbies I do for pure joy. My life has side quests. It stays playful.

**Spirituality:**
I am connected to something larger than my ambitions. I have a practice — yoga, Ayurveda, moments of actual silence. I treat my body as a system I want to understand. I am curious about ancient knowledge. I have a relationship with my own intuition. I listen to it. I have learned to tell the difference between fear and knowing.

**Love:**
I am with someone exceptional. Distractingly handsome, successful, and secure. He chose me publicly and proudly. He is devoted. I arrived at this relationship whole. Our relationship is high energy, passionate, adventurous, and happy. There is passion, desire, real chemistry that makes everything vivid. He is a good person. He shows up for people around him without needing credit. He is generous without an audience. He is principled.

**Thread (The Meta-Vision):**
I become someone whose inner life is as developed as her outer ambition. I write and I publish. Not just reports and memos — I write stories. I am working on a book. People will read it and something in them will shift. My work creates meaningful impact. I am genuinely, undeniably smart, and I use it — not to perform intelligence but to go deep. I have a village. A circle of people who actually know me, show up for me, help me. I am loved by people I have chosen and who have chosen me back.

## The 2026 Goal Achievement Protocol

### Phase 1 — Baseline Assessment

**Year retrospective:** Wins and wins in disguise. What worked vs. what didn't. Where you showed up for yourself vs. where you abandoned yourself. Recurring patterns. What you're not willing to tolerate anymore. Synthesise the year in one word or sentence.

**Life Wheel (1–10):** Personal Growth, Health, Love/Self-Love, Relationships, Leisure, Spirituality, Career, Finance. Lowest scores = blind spots. Not optional.

**Accomplishments inventory:** Two lists — planned and accomplished + unexpected victories. Your brain has negativity bias. Objective data corrects this. Make this monthly.

**Blind spot identification:** Review Life Wheel, get feedback from 3–5 trusted people, review struggles for patterns. Name them explicitly — unacknowledged blind spots = planning failure.

### Phase 2 — Purpose Mapping

**Purpose circles:** Three overlapping circles — what you'd do for free, what you're good at, what the world needs/will pay for. The overlap is your north star.

**Identity statements:** Not "I want to be X" but "I am X." Identity first, goals second.

### Phase 3 — Goal Architecture

**5-year framework.** Long enough for major shifts. Short enough to feel real and urgent.

For each goal: specific, measurable, dated, actionable.

**Physical Health:** Run a half marathon by 2027. 10 unassisted pull-ups by 2028. Exercise 5×/week consistently. Sprint triathlon. Stable energy without afternoon crashes.

**Mental Health:** ADHD medication protocol solidified by 2026. Phone screen time under 3 hours/day. Weekly therapy. Task initiation systems that don't require constant willpower.

**Career:** First role at impact VC or climate finance by 2026. Investment associate level by 2028. Expertise that makes you the obvious referral for a specific thesis.

**Financial:** Emergency fund (6 months). ROTH IRA or equivalent. Clear understanding of personal finances.

**Lifestyle:** 2 international trips per year. Clean, well-designed home. Friendships with clear and respected boundaries. Active creative hobbies. Spiritual practice.
