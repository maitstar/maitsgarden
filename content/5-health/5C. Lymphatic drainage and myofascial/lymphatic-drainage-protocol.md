# Lymphatic Drainage Protocol

## Morning (20 min)

1. **Stretch** (3 min) — cat-cows, slow sun salutations, chest/hip opener. Release tension in the torso.
2. **Breathing** (3 min) — lie down or sit. Inhale through nose, belly rises. Brief pause. Slow exhale. Recruits the diaphragm.
3. **Collar bones** (1 min) — fingertips in small circles above and below both collar bones. More time on the left — that's where most abdominal lymph drains.
4. **Armpits** (1 min) — cup hand and pump gently into each armpit. Also rub circular around the armpit and into surrounding chest/shoulder area.
5. **Groin** (1 min) — fingertips pressing along the crease where leg meets pelvis, both sides. Light circular strokes.
6. **Cisterna chyli** (1 min) — lie down. Just below sternum, slightly left of center. Press in rhythmic pulses, in and release. Breathe deeply while doing this.
7. **Quadrants** (8 min, 2 min each) — divide stomach into 4 sections (two above navel, two below):
   - Upper right: stroke upward and toward right armpit
   - Upper left: stroke upward and toward left armpit
   - Lower right: stroke downward and toward right groin crease
   - Lower left: stroke downward and toward left groin crease
   - Pressure should be barely-there. Moving skin, not kneading muscle.
8. **Movement** (2 min) — jump, shake whole body, heel raises, walk, or rebound.

## Evening (13 min)

1. **Open collarbones** (2 min) — rub, tap, gently massage above and below collarbones. Opens subclavian veins where lymph re-enters bloodstream.
2. **Clear neck & ears** (2 min) — massage down the neck and around jugulodigastric nodes (below earlobe, behind jaw). Gently pull and massage ears and base of skull.
3. **Sculpt jawline** (3 min) — massage underneath jawline and along the bone. Direct fluid downward toward the neck.
4. **Drain mid-face** (4 min):
   - Tap: lightly tap the entire face
   - Target: ring fingers for circular massage on puffy areas (under-eyes, cheeks, chin)
   - Direct: always finish by guiding fluid toward ears and down the neck
5. **Scalp massage** (2 min) — massage entire scalp, top down.
6. **Full body movement** (2 min) — heel taps, jumping, or shaking.

## Key Principles

- Always move fluid **toward the collarbones**
- Keep pressure **light** — lymphatic vessels are superficial
- Left and right sides drain independently — spend more time on whichever feels more swollen
- Apply face oil before evening routine to avoid pulling skin
