# Cardio & Conditioning

**No running.** Primary tools: swimming, walking, rowing. Goal: burn calories and improve cardiovascular fitness without adding thigh bulk or joint stress.

## Weekly Cardio Target

| Type | Frequency | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Swimming | 2–3× | 20–40 min |
| Walking | Daily | 7–8K steps |
| Rowing (optional) | 1–2× | 20–30 min |
| YouTube/other | 1× optional | 20 min |

## Swimming (Primary)
Build to 20 laps continuous. Progress: 5–8 laps weeks 1–2 → 10 laps weeks 3–4 → 15–20 laps weeks 5–8. Freestyle for efficiency; breaststroke for gentler joints.

## Rowing Machine (Secondary)
Posterior-chain focused — supports glutes without adding thigh bulk. 20–30 min steady state OR 10 × 30 sec hard / 90 sec easy intervals.

## Daily Walking (Non-Negotiable)
7,000–8,000 steps/day. Stack naturally: after meals, stairs, calls. 10-min post-meal walks improve blood sugar and digestion.

## Zone 2 Note
Zone 2 cardio (conversational pace, 60–70% max HR) is underrated for fat oxidation and mitochondrial health. Swimming and walking both qualify.
