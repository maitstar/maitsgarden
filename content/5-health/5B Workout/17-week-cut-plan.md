# 17-Week Cut Plan

## Targets

- **Calories:** 1,366 kcal/day → 0.3 kg/week loss (faster: 1,280 kcal → 0.5 kg/week)
- **Macros (35/45/20):** 120g protein / 154g carbs / 30g fat
- **Protein floor:** minimum 55g/day for muscle preservation
- **Fasting:** Start 12:12 → progress to 16:8 (eat 12pm–8pm)
- **Steps:** 7–8K daily

---

## Training Split

| Day | Focus | Key Movements |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Glute-Ham / Posterior Chain | Hip-Thrust 4×8–12, RDL 3×10–12, Band Abduction 3×15–20, Hamstring Curl 3×12–15 |
| Day 2 | Upper Push | Flat DB Press 3×8–12, Incline DB Press 3×8–12, OHP 3×8–12, Face Pull 3×15–20, Tricep Extension 3×12–15 |
| Day 3 | Quad-Light + Core Stability | Goblet Squat 3×10–12, Step-Up 3×10–12, Bulgarian Split-Squat, Plank 3×45–60s, Dead-Bug 3×12–15 |
| Day 4 | Upper Pull | Bent-Over Row 3×8–12, One-Arm Row 3×8–12, Pull-Up 3×max, Bicep Curl 3×12–15, Rear-Delt Fly 3×12–15 |

**Additional cardio:** Rowing 2–3×/week, 20–30 min steady or intervals. Swimming 2×/week.

**Progression rule:** Add 2.5 kg or 2–3 reps each week on every main lift.

**Core note:** Replace weighted side-crunches with plank, side-plank, dead-bug, bird-dog. These pull the waist in without thickening the rectus abdominis.

---

## BMR & TDEE

- BMR = 10×55 + 6.25×158 − 5×27 − 161 = **~1,242 kcal/day**
- TDEE (lightly active, ×1.375) = **~1,708 kcal/day**
- 20% deficit → **1,366 kcal/day**

---

## Supplement & Medication Timing (Fasting-Compatible)

- **During fast:** water, black coffee, herbal tea, creatine, magnesium, glycine, B-vitamins, B12
- **Just before training (still fasted):** beet-based pre-workout (~5–10 kcal)
- **With first meal:** fat-soluble vitamins (D, K, A, E), triphala, Strattera (or with small bite of food if needed earlier)
- **Post-workout (eating window):** protein shake, BCAAs
- **Eating window:** walnut/carrot milk, matcha with oat milk

---

## Protocol Notes

1. Break the fast with a protein-rich meal: tofu scramble, Greek yogurt, or soy protein shake
2. Diet break after 8 weeks: raise to maintenance (~1,708 kcal) for 5–7 days, then resume deficit
3. If lifts drop >5% for two sessions: check protein intake and consider a slight calorie increase

---

## Monitoring

| Metric | Frequency | Action if off-track |
|---|---|---|
| Weight (same-time morning) | Weekly | If stalled >2 weeks: cut ~70–130 kcal or add 10-min rowing |
| Waist & hip circumference | Every 2 weeks | If waist not dropping: tighten deficit or add core volume |
| Strength log | Each session | If lifts drop >5% for 2 sessions: check protein, consider small calorie increase |
| Energy / hunger (1–10) | Daily | If ≤3 consistently: raise by ~100 kcal or shorten fast to 14:10 |
| Sleep | Daily | Aim 7–9 hours |

---

## Expected Timeline

- **Weeks 1–4:** 0.2–0.3 kg/week loss (mostly water/glycogen). Waist feels tighter; strength improves.
- **Weeks 5–8:** Steady fat loss; waist begins to shrink 1–2 in; glutes firmer; WHR moves toward ~0.75.
- **Week 9:** Diet break — common "whoosh" in weight after.
- **Weeks 10–15:** Continue deficit; waist approaching 24–26 in target; body-fat in low-20% range.
- **Beyond 15 weeks:** Insert another diet break or adjust calories/cardio if plateau appears.

---

## Body Goal Targets

| Area | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Waist | 24–26 in (from ~29.5 in) | Moderate deficit + core stability work |
| Hips | 39–41 in (from ~37.5 in) | Hip-dominant lifts with progressive overload |
| Body fat | 18–22% (from ~28.7%) | Calorie deficit + training |
| Thighs | Don't bulk — keep quad volume modest | Prioritize posterior chain over quad-dominant moves |
