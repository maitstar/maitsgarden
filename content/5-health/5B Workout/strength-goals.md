# Strength Goals & Progression

## Strength Minimums (Baseline Competency)
Before chasing aesthetics: hit these functional floors.
- **Pull-ups:** 5 unassisted
- **Squat:** bodyweight (54 kg) for reps
- **Deadlift:** 1.5× bodyweight (~80 kg)
- **Bench press:** 1/3 bodyweight (~18 kg)
- 1 mile in 9 minutes
- 15 pushups
- farmers carry 50% 
- 10 burpees
- touch toes
- 90s plank

These are the minimums, not the goals. Hit them and then push.

## Starting Point
- Body weight: 54 kg | Height: 158 cm

## Lower Body Targets (Priority)

| Lift | Current | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Hip Thrust | 10 kg × 8–12 | 20–25 kg × 8–12 |
| RDL | 10 kg × 10–12 | 20 kg × 10–12 |
| Bulgarian Split Squat | Bodyweight | 5–8 kg DBs |
| Goblet Squat | 4.5 kg avg | 8 kg avg |

## Upper Body (Maintenance)
Upper body is already proportional — maintain rather than bulk. Keep volume moderate, prioritize form and control.

## Progression Rule
Each session: add 0.5 kg to the lift OR add 2–3 reps at same weight. Current average lift 4.5 kg → target 8 kg.

## Training Split
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Glute & Hamstring (priority day) |
| Day 2 | Push (Chest/Shoulders/Triceps) |
| Day 3 | Quad + Core Stability |
| Day 4 | Pull (Back/Biceps/Rear Delts) |

**Glute & Ham day is the shape-change day** — treat it as non-negotiable.

Core focus: stability work (plank, dead-bug, bird-dog) — these pull the waist in without widening the rectus abdominis. No weighted side-crunches.

## Flexibility & Mobility
- 2–3 sessions/week on rest days or post-workout
- Morning mobility (5–10 min): waterfall arm rise, hip circles, cat-cow, leg swings, world's greatest stretch
- Post-workout stretching: pigeon pose, low lunge, standing hamstring, doorway chest, child's pose
- Dedicated yin yoga 1×/week for deep connective tissue work
