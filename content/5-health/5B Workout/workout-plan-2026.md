# Workout Plan 2026

## Targets
- Calories: 1300–1400/day
- Protein: 95g/day
- Lifting: 4x/week
- Zone 2 cardio: 3x/week
- Steps: 8–12k/day
- Duration: 3–4 months

## Weekly Structure
- 3 days heavy lifting — compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses). Forces brain to coordinate complex motor patterns.
- 2 days Zone 2 — 30–45 min brisk walking or light cycling. Brain's waste-clearing system.
- 1–2 days HIIT — most powerful trigger for BDNF. Physically rebuilds frontal cortex.

## Strength Milestones
1. 5 pull-ups
2. Squat bodyweight for 5 reps
3. Deadlift 1.5x bodyweight
4. Bench press 1/3 bodyweight

## Lower Body Circuit
- Leg raise hold
- Scissor kicks
- Leg raises
- Single leg fit
- Flutter kicks
