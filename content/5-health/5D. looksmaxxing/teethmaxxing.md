# Teethmaxxing Protocol

## Brushing
- Brush 2–3x/day for a full 2 minutes each time.
- Removes significantly more plaque than once-a-day brushing.

## Flossing
- Floss 1x/day minimum, ideally at night.
- Bleeding usually stops after 7–14 days of consistency.
- Decay starts between teeth — flossing matters as much as brushing.

## Snacking
- Limit snacking frequency more than reducing total sugar.
- Each snack drops mouth pH for 30–60 minutes.
- Fewer eating windows > constant grazing.

## Saliva
- Healthy saliva flow is one of the strongest predictors of dental health.
- Hydrate consistently and avoid mouth breathing.

## Xylitol Gum
- Chew sugar-free (xylitol) gum 10–20 minutes after meals.
- Boosts saliva and lowers cavity risk.
- Xylitol reduces cavity-causing bacteria.
