# Correcting Facial Asymmetry & Posture

## Posture Corrections (Affects Face)

Address: anterior pelvic tilt, lateral pelvic tilt, flat feet, forward head posture.

Protocol: release fascia → stretch → strengthen. 15-minute routine, 2–3x per week.
- Barefoot sprinting and myofascial training help
- Chin tucking for forward head posture
- Tongue tie stretch + isometric tongue hold + thumb pull to correct palate
- Mouth tape while sleeping. Don't sleep on your back.
- Reference: Oscar Patel on YouTube

## Myofunctional Exercises

Target facial muscles directly. Passive measures (tongue posture, mouth breathing fix) are crucial alongside active exercises.
