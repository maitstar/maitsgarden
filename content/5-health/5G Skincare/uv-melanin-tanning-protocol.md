# UV, Melanin & Tanning Protocol

## How Tanning Works
- UV photons penetrate epidermis and strike melanocytes
- UVA oxidizes existing melanin (immediate darkening)
- UVB stimulates new melanin production
- Skin type 3–4: tans easily, high eumelanin concentration
- Visible light also triggers melanocytes (laptop screens matter)

## Three-Pronged Defense

### 1. Block the photons
- Use sunscreen combining **chemical filters** (absorb UV) + **mineral filters** (reflect UV)
- Mineral filters are better for UVA — always include
- Look for **PA++++** rating
- **La Roche-Posay UV Mune 400** — best filter
- **Eucerin Sun Pigment Control Fluid** — contains Thiamidol (tyrosinase inhibitor, safe during day)
- **Helio Care** — for HEV/visible light protection
- Iron oxide in tinted products/makeup shields visible light

### 2. Suppress the internal signal (tyrosinase inhibitors)
- **Alpha-arbutin**
- **Tranexamic acid**
- **Kojic acid**
- **Niacinamide**

### 3. Speed up skin turnover (remove umbrella skin)
- **AHAs** (e.g., glycolic/lactic acid) — exfoliate to remove darkened keratinocytes

## Antioxidant Undercoat
The most rigorously studied combination:
- **15% L-ascorbic acid** (Vitamin C) — neutralizes free radicals, signals melanocytes
- **1% Vitamin E** — lipid-soluble, protects the membrane barrier
- **0.5% Ferulic acid** — stabilizer, doubles photo-protection of the serum

Best product: **SkinCeuticals C E Ferulic**

## Routine
1. Double cleanse
2. Vitamin C serum (C E Ferulic)
3. Sunscreen (PA++++ mineral + chemical combo)
4. Tinted makeup with iron oxide for HEV shield
5. PM: AHA exfoliant + tyrosinase inhibitor
