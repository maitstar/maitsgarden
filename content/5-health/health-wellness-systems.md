**Direct Support:**
- **Creatine** - Prefrontal cortex is energy-hungry, creatine provides rapid ATP recycling
- **Cold exposure** - Brief cold showers increase norepinephrine, supports working memory and executive function for hours afterward
- **Tyrosine (500-1000mg before high-demand tasks)** - Precursor to dopamine/norepinephrine, supports working memory under stress
- **Phosphatidylserine** - Supports cortical function, reduces cognitive fatigue
**Second-Order Support:**

- **Strategic caffeine + L-theanine** - Just before tasks requiring verbal output, not throughout day (prevents depletion/crash)
**Third-Order Support:**
- **Offload to external systems** - Voice memos instead of holding thoughts, visual cues instead of remembering, scripts for predictable conversations
- **Pre-decision for interviews** - Have 5 prepared stories you can deploy so you're not generating narratives in real-time

## **Sleep Hygiene**
## **Core Principle**
**Wake up same time every day** - Even on weekends
## **Two Things Influence Sleepy and Awake**
1. **Internal 24h clock (circadian rhythm)**
	- Circadian center of brain sits above optic center so can sample light
	- Light exposure in morning sets your rhythm
	- Darkness at night reinforces it
1. **Sleep pressure (chemical build up)**
	- Adenosine builds throughout the day
	- Creates pressure to sleep
	- Cleared during sleep
## **Sleep Optimization**
- **Prioritize sleep** - Use a tracker and optimize your sleep environment
- **Dark, cool, quiet room**
- **No screens 1 hour before bed**
- **Consistent bedtime routine**
- **No caffeine after 3pm**
- **Consider magnesium glycinate before bed**

## **SOS Protocol When Dysregulated**
## **Immediate Reset Techniques**
**Name it out loud:** "I'm having a big reaction to something small. My body thinks this is an emergency but it's not." Sometimes just labeling it creates distance.
**Physical reset:**
- Cold water on your face
- Ice cube in your hand
- Intense physical movement (jumping jacks, push-ups)
- You need to interrupt the physiological spiral
**Box breathing:**
- 4 counts in
- 4 hold
- 4 out
- 4 hold
- Do this for 2-3 minutes. It's boring but it works.
**Time-box the reaction:** "I'm upset. I'm going to be upset for 30 minutes, then I'm going to \[specific next thing\]." Give yourself permission, but with boundaries.

## **To Prevent Losing Entire Days**
**Set a timer:** When you turn to Netflix/scrolling, set a 45-minute timer. When it goes off, reassess. Do you need another 45 minutes, or can you do one small task first?
**One small win:** After you've regulated down a bit, do ONE thing - respond to one email, make your bed, whatever. It breaks the "day is ruined" spiral.
**Write it out:** Angry journal dump for 10 minutes. Get the spiraling thoughts out of your head and onto paper where they can't loop endlessly.

## **Longer-Term Prevention**
**Regular physical release:** You need a way to discharge stress that isn't just coping after the fact. Running, boxing, dancing - something that gets the emotion out of your body.
**Boundaries with family:** Can you limit how much they tell you about astrologer visits, or ask them not to share "predictions" about your life?
**Build stress capacity:**
- Daily meditation
- Regular exercise
- Adequate sleep
- Supportive relationships
- Creative outlets
