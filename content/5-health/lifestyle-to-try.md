## Sleep & Recovery
- Linen or mulberry bed sheets
- Hot bath before bed (drops cortisol)
- Make a sleep perfume or herbal oil
- Don't do anything in your bed besides sleeping
- Buy a CO2 monitor — cognition peaks at 550 ppm baseline
- Mama for sleep: https://x.com/BottleBell/status/2034117628994707605
- Breath work for smaller waist: https://x.com/valorascult/status/2033961649162400169
- Sauna 4–7×/week shows 65% lower risk of Alzheimer's in long-term research
- Take melatonin 3 hours before target bedtime (not right before)
- Have a fixed bedtime
- Need an hour of sunlight exposure every day
- Morning electrolytes before coffee: salt + lemon (also in Biohacking section)
- Sleep WEEK 2: Remove screens + introduce wind-down ritual — no screens 30 min before sleep, warm tea, warm bath, in bed before 10:30 PM
- Sleep WEEK 3: Prepare tomorrow's morning + lock in full evening stack — warm breakfast prep, all 6 evening habits, 8k steps, fermented foods at dinner
