# Breathwork for Smaller Waist

## Protocol (daily, once)

**Step 1 — 3 minutes**
Lie on your back, knees bent, feet on wall at 90° angle. Reach arms toward the wall. Inhale through nose, exhale through mouth as if fogging a mirror. Feel ribs move down and engage pelvic floor.

**Step 2 — 1.5 minutes each side**
Lie on your side, knees bent. Inhale, then on exhale draw waist inward and hold for 8 seconds. Repeat on other side.
