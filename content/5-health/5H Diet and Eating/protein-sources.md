# Vegetarian Protein Sources

Target: **55g minimum → 120g ideal** per day (per 17-week cut plan macros)

| Food | Serving | Calories | Protein | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tempeh | 1 cup (166g) | 320 kcal | 30g | Highest plant protein per cup. Fermented = better gut health. |
| Cottage cheese | 1 cup (226g) | 220 kcal | 25g | Slow-digesting casein — good before bed. |
| Paneer | 1 cup (244g) | 340 kcal | 22g | High protein, high fat — use as main dish, not side. |
| Firm Tofu | 1 cup (248g) | 180 kcal | 20g | Absorbs flavour well. Best scrambled or pan-fried. |
| Greek yogurt (full fat) | 1 cup (245g) | 220 kcal | 20g | Good for gut health. Best at breakfast or as snack. |
| Edamame | 1 cup (155g) | 190 kcal | 17g | Complete protein. Great snack or in salads. |
| Lentils (cooked) | 1 cup (198g) | 230 kcal | 18g | High fibre and iron. Dal is your friend. |
| Hummus | 1 cup (240g) | 410 kcal | 18g | Watch calorie density. |
| Chickpeas (cooked) | 1 cup (164g) | 270 kcal | 15g | Versatile — curries, roasted, hummus. |
| Kidney beans (cooked) | 1 cup (177g) | 225 kcal | 15g | Pair with rice for complete protein. |
| Eggs | 2 large | 140 kcal | 12g | Complete amino acid profile. |
| Hemp seeds | 3 tbsp (30g) | 170 kcal | 10g | Complete protein, high omega-3. Sprinkle on anything. |
| Pumpkin seeds | ¼ cup (30g) | 170 kcal | 9g | Also high zinc and magnesium. |
| Soy milk (fortified) | 1 cup (240ml) | 80–100 kcal | 7g | Best plant milk for protein. |

## Key Rules
- **Complete proteins** (all essential amino acids): tofu, tempeh, edamame, eggs, paneer, Greek yogurt, hemp seeds, cottage cheese
- **Complementary combos:** lentils + rice, chickpeas + whole wheat — pair across the day
- **Post-workout:** tofu, paneer, eggs, or protein shake within ~90 min of training
- **Budget-best:** lentils and chickpeas for protein-per-rupee value
- Aim to have protein in **every meal** to hit the 120g target
