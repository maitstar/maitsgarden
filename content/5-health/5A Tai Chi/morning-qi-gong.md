1. **“Waterfall” Arm Rise**
	- Inhale deeply; raise arms slowly in front of you to shoulder height, palms up.
	- Exhale; lower arms like pouring water from a jug, fingers trailing.
	- *Reps:* 6‑8 slow cycles.
	- *Why it helps:* Warms shoulders, spine, and elbows; increases upper‑body fluidity . 
1. **“Bowing to Heaven and Earth”**
	- From standing, hinge at hips; reach arms overhead, then let them hang toward the floor.
	- Bend knees slightly; gaze up, then down to toes, letting spine roll gently.
	- *Reps:* 5 slow hinging movements.
	- *Why it helps:* Stretches hamstrings, lower back, and calves; improves spinal mobility . 
1. **“Rolling the Ball” Hip Opener**
	- Stand with feet shoulder‑width apart.
	- Slowly roll hips forward, up, back, and down in a big circle, arms flowing in sync.
	- Reverse direction.
	- *Reps:* 5 circles each way.
	- *Why it helps:* Releases hip and leg tightness; enhances hip mobility crucial for squats/hip‑thrusts . 
1. **“Wrist and Ankle Flossing”**
	- Lift arms; rotate wrists in circles (10 clocks, 10 counters).
	- Lift legs; rotate ankles in circles (10 each direction).
	- *Reps:* 10 slow rotations.
	- *Why it helps:* Boosts circulation, prevents stiffness, supports joint health . 
1. **“Two Hands Moving Heaven” Spine Stretch**

	- *Reps:* 5‑6 reach cycles.
	- *Why it helps:* Lengthens spine, opens shoulders, combats desk‑hunch and low‑back tension . 
1. **“Pigeon‑Style Lunge”**

	- *Why it helps:* Targets hip flexors and glutes; reduces hip‑dip stress .

**1. Seated Neck Rolls (1–2 minutes)**
- Sit or lie back comfortably.
- Inhale; gently roll your head to the right, down, and left in a half‑moon path.
- Reverse direction.
- *Why it helps:* Releases neck tension from sitting, lifting, and Strattera‑related tightness . 
**2. Seated Cat‑Cow Spine Flow (2–3 minutes)**
- Sit cross‑legged, palms on knees.
- Inhale: arch spine, lift chest, look up (Cow).
- Exhale: round spine, tuck chin, press belly in (Cat).
- Repeat 8–10 times.
- *Why it helps:* Mobilizes spine, improves posture for lifts, and calms the nervous system . 
**3. Reclined Twist (1–2 minutes)**
- Lie on your back, knees bent.
- Cross one knee over the other, gently lower both knees to the side, arms out‑wide.
- Turn your head opposite.
- Repeat on the other side.
- *Why it helps:* Eases lower back and hip tension from squats, hip‑thrusts, and rowing . 
**4. Reclined Pigeon / Glute Pull (1–2 minutes)**
- Lie on your back, knees bent.
- Cross one ankle over the opposite knee (knee‑to‑chest style).
- Gently pull that knee toward your chest.
- Switch sides.
- *Why it helps:* Stretches glutes, hips, and reduces hip‑dip tightness . 
**5. Legs‑Up‑The‑Wall or Reclined Legs (2–3 minutes)**
- Lie on your back, hips close to a wall; lift legs up against it (or just extend legs straight up).
- Let your lower back settle into the floor, hands on belly.
- *Why it helps:* Reduces leg swelling, lowers heart rate, and improves sleep quality .  
**6. Child’s Pose or Deep Forward Fold (1–2 minutes)**
- Kneel; sit back on heels, torso draped over thighs, arms forward.
- Or, stand and fold at hips, letting arms hang.
- *Why it helps:* Calms the mind, stretches hips, hamstrings, and back; great before bed . 
**7. Final Deep Breathing (1–2 minutes)**
- Lie flat, legs straight or knees bent, hands on ribs.
- Inhale for 4 counts, exhale for 6 counts.
- Repeat 5–10 rounds.
- *Why it helps:* Shifts into “rest‑and‑digest,” aids recovery, and supports sleep under calorie deficit and fasting
