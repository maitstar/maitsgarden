# Wind-Down & Evening Ritual

## Sequence

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ~6:00–6:30 PM | Evening walk | 20–30 min — decompresses nervous system, counts toward step goal |
| ~7:00 PM | Read | Physical book only |
| ~7:30–8:00 PM | Dinner | Protein-forward, lighter than lunch. Eat by 8PM. |
| After dinner | Short walk or gentle stretch | 10 min — aids digestion, clears the mind |
| ~8:30 PM | Meditate | 5–10 min |
| ~8:45 PM | Hot shower | The anchor — lowers core temp, triggers sleep onset |
| After shower | Abhyanga | Warm sesame or coconut oil. Grounding. Calms the nervous system. |
| After shower | Skincare routine | Brush teeth, wash face, evening skincare |
| ~9:30 PM | Evening journaling | Emotional download — what happened, what you feel, what you're releasing |
| ~9:40 PM | Plan for tomorrow | Write top 3 priorities — closes the mental loop so brain rests |
| ~9:50 PM | Visualise | 5 min lying in bed — tomorrow going well, a goal, something you want to feel |
| ~10:00 PM | Sleep | Eye mask on, AC at 23°C |

## Principles
- The shower is the anchor — if nothing else, do this
- Journaling + tomorrow's plan = mental closure (stops the brain "running" overnight)
- No screens from ~8:30 PM onward for best sleep onset
- Abhyanga is optional but powerful on high-stress days

## Sleep Supplements
Taken before bed: magnesium, glycine, lysine (immune + sleep quality), triphala (gut health)

## Avoid After 8 PM
Caffeine (obviously), heavy food, bright overhead lights, emotionally activating content

## Morning Light Counterpart
Get 10–20 min of outdoor sunlight within 30–60 min of waking — no sunglasses, no phone, no glass window. This anchors the circadian rhythm and makes the 10PM bedtime achievable. On Kerala's cloudy days, 15–20 min still works (outdoor light is 10–50× brighter than indoor even through overcast).
