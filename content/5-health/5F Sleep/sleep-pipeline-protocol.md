# Sleep Pipeline Protocol

## Goal
Optimize melatonin production without relying on high-dose supplements.

## Upstream Precursors (feed the pipeline)
- **5-HTP** (50–100mg, evening) — skips the tryptophan step, more direct. Don't take long-term without breaks.
- OR **Tryptophan** (500mg with a carb-heavy meal) if 5-HTP feels too strong.

## Enzyme Cofactors (make the pathway run)
- **Vitamin B6 as P5P** (25–50mg) — critical cofactor at two steps. Not regular pyridoxine.
- **Zinc** (15–30mg with food) — cofactor for ASMT, the final step. Take away from magnesium.
- **Magnesium glycinate** (300–400mg, evening) — supports GABA, reduces cortisol that suppresses pineal output. Take with walnut (fat-soluble).

## Suppress What Blocks Melatonin
- **Ashwagandha KSM-66** (300–600mg) — cortisol suppression. High cortisol at night directly inhibits pineal output.
- **Light discipline** — blue light after sunset wrecks AANAT activity regardless of what you take.

## Direct Melatonin Support
- **Low-dose melatonin** (0.3mg, not 5–10mg) — pharmacological doses cause receptor downregulation. 0.3mg mimics physiological levels. KLAC: let go of melatonin in high doses.

## Food Shortcuts
- **Pistachios** — highest natural melatonin content of any food
- **Tart cherry concentrate** — meaningful melatonin + anthocyanins supporting AANAT
- **Eggs + pumpkin seeds** — tryptophan density

## Timing Habits
- Sauna and heat in the second half of the day
- Watch sunset to signal eyes that it's time to sleep
- Early bed times (KLAC: add)

## Pre-Sleep Routine
- Deep tummy breath, 360°
- Side bends
- Twist
- Massage: Anmian, Third Eye, HT7, PC6
