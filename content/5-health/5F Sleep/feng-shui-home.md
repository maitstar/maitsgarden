# Feng Shui & Home Energy Optimization

## Clutter as Stuck Energy

Clutter represents unfinished business, unresolved emotions, past identities you haven't released, and decisions you've been avoiding. Physical clutter creates mental clutter — you cannot think clearly in a chaotic space.

## Bagua Map — Clutter Effects by Area

| Area | Life Domain | Effect of Clutter |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Wealth (SE) | Prosperity | Money feels stuck, opportunities don't flow |
| Fame (S) | Reputation | Hard to be seen, career stalls |
| Love (SW) | Relationships | Loneliness, difficulty attracting/keeping love |
| Family (E) | Health, new beginnings | Family tension, hard to start fresh |
| Center | Overall wellbeing | General overwhelm, scattered energy |
| Children (W) | Creativity, projects | Creative blocks, projects don't complete |
| Knowledge (NE) | Wisdom | Hard to learn, decisions feel foggy |
| Career (N) | Life path | Career drift, no clear direction |
| Helpful People (NW) | Mentors, support | No support system showing up |

## Special Storage Rules

- **Under the bed:** Keep only soft items (linens, clothing). No shoes, sharp objects, emotionally charged items — stores in subconscious during sleep.
- **Corners:** Energy stagnates. Add a plant, light, or moving object.
- **Entryway:** First impression for energy. Must be clear. No shoe pile-up, no dead plants.
- **Closets:** Hidden clutter still affects energy. Clear seasonally.

## Clearing Rules

1. Remove everything you don't use, love, or that has no clear purpose
2. Fix broken things immediately or throw them out
3. Return borrowed items; retrieve your own
4. Release gifts you feel obligated to keep but don't love
5. Clear one area at a time

## Arrangement Principles

**Command position:** Bed should have a clear view of the door but not be directly in line with it.

**Pairs for love:** Arrange items in pairs in the bedroom and love corner (SW) — two candles, two pillows, two nightstands. Single items signal single life.

**Flow:** Furniture must not block doorways. Move freely through every room.

**Five elements:**
- Wood: growth (east, family)
- Fire: passion, visibility (south, fame)
- Earth: stability (center, NE, SW)
- Metal: clarity (west, NW)
- Water: wisdom, flow (north, career)

## Four-Box Clearing Method

For each clearing session: Keep / Relocate (belongs elsewhere in home) / Donate-Sell / Trash. Work quickly — trust first instinct.
