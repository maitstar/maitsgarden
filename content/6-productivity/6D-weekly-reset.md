# Weekly Reset

Pick one day per week — Sunday works well. Not a production day: a reset day. The reset is what makes every other day run better; skip it and entropy builds.

## Reset Day Flow

| Block | Activity |
|---|---|
| Morning | Sleep in slightly. Slower morning. No rushing. |
| Morning | Brain dump — clear mental RAM |
| Mid-morning | Weekly review: what happened vs. what was planned |
| Mid-morning | Weekly planning: set intentions and priorities |
| Afternoon | Admin: emails, organise files, clear workspace |
| Afternoon | Body admin: laundry, groceries, supplement restock |
| Late afternoon | Longer walk or swim — physical reset |
| Evening | Optional: reading, face mask, extra self-care |
| Evening | Extended wind-down. Earlier bedtime than usual. |

## Opening Ritual (Set the Tone)
1. Make a special drink — matcha, herbal tea, something reserved for reset day
2. Clear physical space before starting — tidy desk = tidy mind
3. Light a candle or put on specific music only used for reset (Pavlovian cue)
4. Set intention: *"I am clearing space to start fresh"*

## Brain Dump Questions (~15 min)

**Clearing mental load:**
- What's taking up space in my head right now?
- What am I worried about or avoiding?
- What's unfinished and nagging at me?
- What promises have I made to myself or others that I haven't kept?
- What do I need to say to someone but haven't?
- Is there anything I'm pretending is fine when it isn't?
- What would I do differently if I could reset this week?

**Ideas & captures:**
- What ideas have I had this week that I haven't written down?
- What did I read, watch, or hear that stuck with me?
- What creative ideas are floating around that I don't want to lose?
- Is there a problem I've been thinking about that I haven't articulated yet?
- What's one thing I want to experiment with or try?

**Relationships:**
- Who have I been thinking about that I haven't reached out to?
- Is there a relationship that needs attention or repair?
- Who do I want to spend more time with? Less time with?
- Did anyone help me this week that I haven't thanked?

**Body & health:**
- How did my body feel this week overall?
- What did I eat that I'm proud of? What am I not proud of?
- Did I move enough? Sleep enough?
- Is there anything my body is trying to tell me?

**Inner life:**
- What emotions have I been carrying this week?
- What made me happy? What drained me?
- Am I living in alignment with my values right now?
- What do I need more of? Less of?
- What would make me feel proud by next Sunday?

## Weekly Review (~20–30 min)
- What did I actually do this week?
- What went well? What didn't?
- What didn't get done — does it still matter?
- What drained me? What energised me?
- What do I want to do differently?

## Weekly Planning Questions (~20 min)

**Big picture:**
- Am I moving toward what matters most right now?
- What's the one thing that, if I do it this week, makes everything else easier?
- Is there anything to deprioritise or drop entirely?

**Top 3 priorities (write them):**

2. 
3. 
What does "done" look like for each? Which day will I do each?

**Career/work:** Applications, follow-ups, prep, learning, networking — what's the most important move this week?

**Health:** Workout schedule, fasting window, hardest habit to protect, anything to prep?

**Creative/writing:** Any writing scheduled? Minimum commitment? Any ideas to develop?

**Self & rest:** When will I have real downtime? Who do I want to connect with? What am I looking forward to?

**Logistics:** Admin, payments, errands, appointments, anything needing advance booking?

**Closing question:** *If this week goes really well, what will have happened?* Write that down. That's the target.

## Weekly Reset Checklist

**Mental/Digital:**
- [ ] Brain dump complete
- [ ] Last week reviewed
- [ ] Next week planned — top 3 per day or per area
- [ ] Email inbox cleared/processed
- [ ] Task list reviewed — archive completed, update trackers

**Physical/Home:**
- [ ] Laundry
- [ ] Tidy workspace and desk
- [ ] Grocery list for the week
- [ ] Supplement stocks checked
- [ ] Workout plan reviewed for the week

**Body:**
- [ ] Weigh in and log measurements (if measurement week)
- [ ] Nutrition reviewed — anything to adjust?
- [ ] Longer walk, swim, or gentle yoga

**Career (if job searching):**
- [ ] Applications reviewed and followed up
- [ ] 1–2 new applications or research sessions
- [ ] Networking messages drafted/sent

## To Try / Systems to Implement

- Daily visibility list — write down what made you visible that day
- Weekly audit: life wheel + 80/20 (what worked, what didn't) + weekly highlight
- Graceful reflection
- Weekly learnings of the week
- What are my operational values vs marketing values?
- Have an experience tracker
- Attend a public lecture
- Journal idea: things I can't change right now vs things I can do right now
- Make punch cards for recurring rewards
- Journal: what's probable, possible, plausible, preferable — for each goal
- Katy Jessel journaling method
- Put bird song as alarm
- Think of skills as development and stimulation (not obligations)
- Write down what matters today, what would make today a win
- On prioritising tasks: do the shortest task first — with two exceptions: stalled tasks get a priority bump; content-dependent tasks override
- 5 things you want to accomplish this week / this month / this year
- Do one thing you've been dreading every week
- Estimate time whenever you make a todo list
- Be intentional — catch the exact moment your brain decides to switch tasks
- Schedule regular periods where your mind can wander freely — walks without podcasts, showers without mental to-do lists
- Identify your collection buckets, empty them weekly
- ADHD tax — the invisible daily cost ADHD charges you in time, money, energy
- Make homework assignments for the week

## Planning Structure

**Month:**
- Set theme, identity anchor, and top priorities
- Create metrics to measure success in each priority
- Identify 3 things that can derail me this month

**Week:**
- Set theme, intent, and 3–5 outcomes
- Fill weekly scorecard
- Create a minimum viable week — what must happen for this to be a good week

**Productivity principles:**
- Live according to desire paths — follow the routes you actually use, not the ones you're supposed to
- You need novelty when you are burnt out
- Weekly review: did I keep my commitments this week?
- Do chores while on the phone
- Make a list of 300 things God would give you (desire priming)
- Have a ritual for the perigee and apogee of the moon
- No phone till 1pm
- Cleaning schedule for the week
