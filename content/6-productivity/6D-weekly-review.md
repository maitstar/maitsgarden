## RELIABILITY ✅
**Core Weekly Behaviors:**
- \[ \] Sunday: Write down 3 key commitments for the week with deadlines
- \[ \] Daily: Reply to all messages within 24 hours (yes/no/timeline)
- \[ \] Day before important event/deadline: Have work 90% ready, review prep
- \[ \] One check-in: Update someone who's waiting on something (proactive update)
- \[ \] Friday: Confirm you kept 100% of your promises this week

**Things NOT to do:**
- \[ \] Don't overcommit to prove reliability (honest no > flaky yes)
- \[ \] Don't ghost on small asks (respond even if "can't do this")
- \[ \] Don't accept work with unclear deadlines

## EXCELLENCE ⭐
**Core Weekly Behaviors:**
- \[ \] Pick ONE deliverable to spend 30% more time on (quality focus)
- \[ \] Before shipping: Self-edit, catch 3+ errors/improvements yourself
- \[ \] Get feedback: Send one piece of work to someone you respect
- \[ \] Study excellence: Spend 30 min analyzing work you admire (ask: what makes this good?)
- \[ \] Review: Check one past piece of work—notice improvement from 6 months ago

**Things NOT to do:**
- \[ \] Don't ship work you'd be embarrassed about
- \[ \] Don't confuse "done" with "good"
- \[ \] Don't compare your behind-the-scenes to others' highlight reel

## PREPAREDNESS 🛡️
**Core Weekly Behaviors:**
- \[ \] Sunday prep: Before week starts, list what you need (materials, context, conversations) for 3 key events
- \[ \] Pre-mortem: For anything with stakes, ask "What could go wrong?" and write 3 things
- \[ \] One backup plan: Write it down (Plan B for something risky)
- \[ \] Buffer time: Add 20% extra time to every estimate; add 1 extra item to prep bag
- \[ \] Review: After 1 event, check—were you prepared? What would you prep differently?

**Things NOT to do:**
- \[ \] Don't over-prepare (analysis paralysis kills momentum)
- \[ \] Don't prepare for unlikely scenarios at expense of likely ones
- \[ \] Don't wing it on big moments—have basics covered
