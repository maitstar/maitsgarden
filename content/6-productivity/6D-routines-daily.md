## **Power Down Ritual (30 Minutes)**
**End of workday routine:**
**1. Clear Open Loops (10 minutes)**
- Go through emails, messages, to-do lists
- Respond to anything urgent (<2 minutes)
- Schedule or add everything else to master list
**2. Review Your Goal Flywheel/Stack (5 minutes)**
- Briefly look at weekly goals
- Remind yourself of bigger picture
**3. Line Up Tomorrow's Dominoes (10 minutes)**
- Identify 1-3 most critical, goal-directed actions for tomorrow
- Write them down on sticky note or prominent place
**4. Organize Your Workspace (3 minutes)**
- Tidy desk
- Prepare everything needed for first task tomorrow
- Remove friction
**5. Verbally Shut Down (2 minutes)**
- Say specific phrase out loud: **"Work is done"**
