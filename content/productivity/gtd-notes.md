# Getting Things Done — Notes

Open loops — thoughts that haven't been brought to closure — interrupt your flow constantly. Your brain will keep reminding you about them whether you want it to or not.

## The Five Steps

1. **Capture** — Instead of keeping ideas, to-dos, and decisions in your head, capture them in some external platform (paper or digital)
2. **Clarify** — What is each item and what can you do with it?
3. **Organize** — Structure the outcomes into a system of lists
4. **Reflect** — Review what is important to you; review the items in your system
5. **Engage** — Pick which action to undertake at this time and do it

## Natural Planning

1. Purpose and principles
2. Outcome visioning
3. Brainstorming
4. Organizing
5. Identifying next actions

## Calendar Items (Cal Newport framework)

- Time-specific actions (e.g., doctor's appointment)
- Day-specific actions (e.g., calling a colleague on her last day before vacation)
- Day-specific information (e.g., files to bring to the appointment)

## Altitude Horizons

- **Ground — Current actions:** Your current tasks, reminders, emails to answer
- **Horizon 1 — Current projects:** On your Projects list; generate most ground-level items
- **Horizon 2 — Areas of Focus and Accountabilities:** The roles and domains you maintain (leadership, strategic planning, health, finances, family)
- **Horizon 3 — One- to two-year goals:** Where you want to be in 1–2 years (e.g., key promotion)
- **Horizon 4 — Long-term visions:** Three- to five-year timeframe; career aspirations, family, external factors
- **Horizon 5 — Life purpose:** Why you exist. All previous horizons are influenced by your answer
