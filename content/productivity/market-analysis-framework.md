# Market Analysis — Meta-Structure Framework

A reusable scaffold for mapping any market. Input: "Map the market for [X]."

## Phase 1: Define the Boundaries

- Core definition: what's included/excluded?
- Adjacent spaces: what are you not mapping but should acknowledge?
- Time horizon: current state, 5-year, 10-year?

## Phase 2: Segmentation (choose 2–3)

**Supply-side:** by technology/production pathway, feedstock/input type, value chain position (developer vs producer vs integrator)

**Demand-side:** by end-use sector, customer type, job-to-be-done, geography/regulatory regime

**Maturity:** by TRL/commercialization stage, cost competitiveness, market adoption (pilots vs early commercial vs scaling)

## Phase 3: Core Analysis Modules

**Module 1 — Technology/Product Landscape:** variants within the space, performance comparison matrix, technical maturity and bottlenecks, differentiation factors

**Module 2 — Economics & Business Models:** cost structures (capex/opex), revenue models, unit economics, margin distribution across value chain, sensitivity analysis

**Module 3 — Competitive Landscape:** player mapping, strategic positioning (incumbents vs startups, horizontal vs vertical), funding landscape, partnership/M&A activity

**Module 4 — Market Drivers & Barriers:** demand drivers, regulatory tailwinds/headwinds, infrastructure constraints, supply chain dependencies, adoption catalysts

**Module 5 — Market Sizing:** TAM/SAM/SOM by segment, growth trajectories and inflection points, scenario analysis (bull/base/bear)

**Module 6 — Climate/Impact Dimension** (when relevant): emissions reduction potential, lifecycle analysis, additionality and integrity concerns, co-benefits and trade-offs

## Phase 4: Synthesis

Produce: executive summary (3–5 key insights), investment thesis or strategic recommendation, risks and open questions, data gaps flagged explicitly.
