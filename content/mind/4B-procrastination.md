#Procrastination : voluntary delay of intended action despite expecting to be worse of for the delay

- Another type: delay due to emotional troubles;
- cost us more than loss of time- well-being, performance, poor health outcomes
- Procrastination comes from overwhelm

## ADHD Task Techniques

- **Embodied tasking** — approach tasks through the body, not just the mind
- **Externalise direction** — lower cognitive load by making the next step physically visible (sticky note, open tab, object in path)
- **Task staging** — make the environment more approachable before starting; also called task costuming: set the scene like a ritual
- **Task costing** — reframe the task with an identity: "pretend you're a world-famous violinist preparing to play"
- **INCUP** — (research what this stands for; captured as an ADHD productivity framework)
- When you avoid a task: ask "how can I make this more enjoyable?"
