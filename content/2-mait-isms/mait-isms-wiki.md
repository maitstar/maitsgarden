# Mait-isms — Personal Philosophy

These are your own words. The things you figured out. They go here so you don't forget them.

## On Career & Work

"In careers, you are either an investor, an operator or a manager." — [[career-principles]]

Work loudly: work on visible projects, projects with quantitative outcomes on an important outcome, never do extra work without making it known.

Tailor your resume, cover letter, and interview persona so completely for each role that you have no fixed form. For a startup, you are a scrappy generalist. For a large corporation, you are a polished specialist. Be what they need you to be. — [[job-search-system]]

Act like a peer, never a subordinate.

You need to be elite at one skill and top 25% in the other to be a prize.

Become Professionally German: punctuality is sacred, structure everything, winging it = death.

Get coffee chats with every single person on the leadership team or professors.

Never assume your coworkers know what's going on — over communicate.

## On Time & Focus

You knock out daily tasks in the first 2 hours, weekly tasks in the first 2 days, and monthly stuff in the first 2 weeks. That way, you stay ahead and don't let things pile up.

Rush to your first deep work session — use fresh morning brain power for your most important work. Aim to start within 20 minutes of waking.

Use 90-minute blocks for planning — deep focus, meetings, and even sleep cycles work in ~90-minute intervals. Plan your day around 5 maximum blocks.

Your capacity for excellence is inversely proportional to the number of your commitments.

In order to be the master of anything, you have to be selfish with your time.

If a task takes under 5 minutes, do it immediately — the mental overhead of tracking small tasks costs more than completing them.

Whenever you get a new task, instead of just putting it on a to-do list, mentally estimate how much time it takes, and immediately put it into your calendar as 3 blocks.

## On People & Social Dynamics

Classify people: Rational, Fragile, Explosive, Strategic. Research what people value — status, results, impact.

People will believe whatever allows them to be the hero of their own story.

Nothing gives you a clearer look into someone than how they misinterpret things — every misinterpretation is a confession.

People subconsciously timestamp every promise you make. Each late delivery moves the clock forward — eventually you exist in their future, not present. Guard punctuality.

Mirror the people in the circles you want to infiltrate.

Never burn a bridge.

In arguments or drama, concede subtly. It ends the fight and makes others question their righteousness.

The art of signaling is subtlety. People connect the dots on their own. Once you make it too explicit, it becomes cringe.

A traditional etiquette rule most have forgotten: don't contradict — experiment with not correcting anyone in social settings.

Don't share negative news from media with others as discussion points unless it's something they must know.

We become the stories others tell about us; if you want someone to do something, describe someone as something.

People don't abandon the people they love, they abandon the people they were using.

## On Identity & Becoming

Procrastination is how you fulfil the fantasy of your potential. — [[adhd-system]]

Seize the means of your life rather than letting life happen to you.

Don't treat life like a transitory period.

Become a serious person. Most people are not serious people and most people do not meet serious people.

Cherrypick your qualities. Discard the useless.

What is a character flaw if not neurological.

Build credibility with yourself.

Use the gifts you were given in this lifetime.

There's a palpable urgency in the air that if you're ever going to chase your life's work the time to do it is now because five years from now the way the world works may look nothing like today.

One of the last remaining vestiges of personal privacy is not telling people what you're going to do before you do it.

Your INTENTION will narrow your focus into the lane & the necessities of the lane will carve you into exactly who you need to be to get there.

## On Love & Relationships

Finding love and connection (friendship included) is truly about exposing your authentic insides to the world and seeing not who glances but stops dead in their tracks.

If a man loves you, the only thing he'll need is for you to exist.

If they won't, someone else will.

Security kills fear of rejection. Positive-sum means genuinely wanting others to win. This combo is magnetic.

Men really respect when you say you have pilates in the morning.

Guys end up in relationships with you because "you really like them" — make sure they're only there because they genuinely love you.

Accept the impermanence of relationships.

You trying to psychoanalyze them for having an avoidant attachment style meanwhile they have an anxious attachment style with someone else.

You have to know what you want, and how you want to be treated or what you deserve. You'll never stick up for yourself if you don't know why you're saying something.

## On Motivation & Urgency

"Pressed to meet the promise of the day — GO." — the only alarm you need.

## On Mind & Processing

This is what this is, this is what this means to me, and this is what I'm doing about it. Process all open loops through this method so they die in your notepad and are reborn as clear, pragmatic steps forward. — [[open-loops]]

Morning pages when half conscious — answers will come. — [[routines-and-systems]]

Never hold on to a thought — write it down.

Treat emotions like visitors who are just passing through.

Figure out how to sit with discomfort.

Reframe "things I'm bad at" to "things I can't do on my own."

Luck = high visibility × active pursuit / high volume

Prized commodity of a community is cool people.

Every single deeply good thing in life comes from sacrificing freedom. Freedom is optionality, a currency to be spent.

## On Body & Appearance

Make sure you're interesting to look at — that's how you become beautiful.

Be attentive to details in all areas of your life — how you plate your food, your nails, etc.

The things you do every day shapes the body you have.

Keep your space clean and uncluttered.

Keep your bed for sleep and intimacy only — no work, no meals. Strengthens sleep associations.

Eat two main meals: heavy breakfast at 10am, dinner before 7pm. Include fruit at 2pm. Reduces blood sugar crashes.
