## Summary
A list of 33 practices for increasing synchronicity and luck in life. Emphasizes presence, divergence from routine, embodied action, and analog practices over digital ones. Core posture: stop optimizing, start showing up differently.

## Key Claims
- Diverge from usual paths — novelty creates unexpected connections
- Narrate your life — externalizing experience makes it legible and shareable
- Do things the moment you think of them — reduces activation energy and captures impulse
- Stop being "good" — follow instinct over propriety
- Make things with your hands without multitasking — embodied focus generates ideas
- Go be among people — passive presence counts
- Write things down (pen & paper > texting) — analog capture changes how you process
- Clean your house when stuck — environmental reset as cognitive reset
- Build one small habit — leverage through consistency
- Pick an archetype and embody it — identity-level behavior change
- Adorn yourself — external signals shift internal state
- Keep a synchronicity log — attention to coincidence increases its frequency
- Talk to yourself in second person — creates observer distance

## Key Concepts

- Synchronicity log — deliberate tracking of coincidences to amplify pattern recognition
