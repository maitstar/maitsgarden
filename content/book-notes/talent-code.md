# The Talent Code — Notes

All skills are based on the same cellular mechanism: the production of myelin around neural pathways.

Much like a wider road allows you to drive faster, a thicker layer of myelin enables electrical impulses to travel more rapidly through a circuit. The thicker the myelin, the greater your ability to control movements and thoughts more accurately.

Making mistakes fosters talent because it stimulates myelin growth.

Analyzing talent hotbeds worldwide reveals talent to be a mixture of:
1. Deep practice
2. Ignition
3. Master coaching

**Deep practice** consists of "chunking up" the task — repeating it and looking for difficulties.

**Ignition** — we need an external cue to motivate us to develop a skill over the long term.
