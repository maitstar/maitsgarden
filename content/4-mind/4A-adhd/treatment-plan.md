[[Wellbutrin Responder Phenotype]]


##  PROFILE 

|Category|Description|
|---|---|
|**Core traits**|Low motivation, poor task initiation, high fatigue, cognitive fog, emotional flatness, poor reward anticipation, low distractibility (not anxious)|
|**Biological pattern**|Low tonic dopamine and norepinephrine; underactive PFC (executive), ACC (effort), and ventral striatum (reward)|
|**Typical presentation**|“I can do things if someone’s watching or there’s a deadline, but otherwise I can’t start”; “Nothing feels worth the effort”; “I feel numb and tired.”|
