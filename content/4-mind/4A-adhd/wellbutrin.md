[[Wellbutrin]] primarily:
- Inhibits [[**norepinephrine (NE)**]] and [[**dopamine (DA)**]] reuptake (so it boosts motivation, focus, and drive), (Wellbutrin’s job is basically to **keep those two signals around longer** in your brain by slowing down their reuptake — like blocking a vacuum cleaner that normally sucks them back up.)
- Has **no direct serotonergic** activity (unlike SSRIs),
- Also acts as a **nicotinic receptor antagonist**, which can improve mood and reduce addictive cravings.

So, people who naturally have _lower baseline dopamine or norepinephrine activity_ — genetically or otherwise — often respond _especially well_ to it.
