# Reflections & Hard Truths

## The Lie to Yourself Principle

"Above all, avoid lies, all lies, especially the lie to yourself. Keep watch on your own lie and examine it every hour, every minute. And avoid contempt, both of others and of yourself: what seems bad to you in yourself is purified by the very fact that you have noticed it in yourself."

The greatest source of suffering is the lies we tell ourselves — pretending you don't need rest, saying "just one more episode," convincing yourself unhealthy relationships are fine, committing to "starting tomorrow."

## Hard Truths

- You're not a people pleaser. You're obsessed with people liking you.
- You're not burnt out. You're distracted.
- You're not actually doing the best you can.
- Stop telling people your plans — you're getting a dopamine hit from saying that.
- Stop breaking promises to yourself.
- Stop orienting your life around judgment and outrage.

## Theory of Self

**Identity is built through repeated action, not discovered through introspection.** You don't find yourself. You build yourself through what you do repeatedly.

**Every day you don't choose is a choice to stay the same.** Not deciding is a decision. Every day you don't apply = choosing unemployment. Don't work out = choosing to stay out of shape. Don't have the hard conversation = choosing dysfunction.

**Jet Lag Theory:** Your current life is the compound result of the past 90 days. Different results require different consistent actions now.

**Engineer the Default:** Willpower is finite. Make good choices automatic; make bad choices require effort.

**Stack momentum before going hard.** Start small. One change at a time. Build the habit before increasing intensity.

**Theme days eliminate switching cost.** Each context switch costs 20–30 minutes of cognitive capacity.

## The Growing Up Declaration

"No one is coming to save me. I must save myself. I want to be an active participant in my own life."

"If I'm not going to die pretty, I want to have lived a life which meant something."

## Relationship Reflections

In good relationships you don't have to perform. Silence is comfortable. Just existing together is enough.

**Live your life like you'll never get married.** Build a life so full that marriage would be optional: career that fulfills, friendships that sustain, financial independence, a home you love.

## Personal Standards

- Show up 100% or don't show up at all. Half-assing wastes everyone's time.
- Always be on offense. Initiate. Create. Move first.

## Energy & Environment

You are shaped by what you absorb — visually, mentally, emotionally. Ruthlessly edit your online experience. Unfollow accounts that make you feel bad. Stagnant energy = stagnant life.

## Astrology: Life Mission

You're the translator in a world that speaks too many languages — bridging complicated ideas and everyday people. Your roadblock isn't understanding. It's speaking up. Words are your tools, not shields. Use them boldly.
