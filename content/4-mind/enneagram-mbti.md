# Personality — Enneagram & MBTI

## Enneagram: Type 4 — The Dreamer / Individualist

**Core avoidance:** being ordinary, rejection, insignificance
**Focus of attention:** what's missing, the past, an idealized future
**Core vice:** envy
**Defense mechanism:** introjection (absorbing others' emotions as your own)

**At best:** expressive, introspective, empathetic, original, depth-seeking
**Under stress:** moody, withdrawn, self-absorbed, prone to comparing unfavorably to others

### Wings
- **4w3 (The Aristocrat):** more ambitious, image-conscious, driven, competitive — wants to be seen as successful and unique
- **4w5 (The Bohemian):** more intellectual, withdrawn, creative in a cerebral way — prioritizes depth and authenticity over recognition

### Growth Direction (toward Type 1)
Integrating structure, self-discipline, and principled action. The healthy 4 stops waiting to feel ready and starts acting from values.

### Stress Direction (toward Type 2)
Under pressure, may become over-focused on others' needs, approval-seeking, or manipulative through emotional sensitivity.

### Personalized Advice
- Perfectionism is a mask — "good enough and shipped" beats "perfect and in my head"
- The comparison trap is the core enemy: someone else's success does not diminish yours
- Your emotional sensitivity is a strength in relationships and creative work; it's a liability in decision-making under pressure
- Build external accountability structures — your internal motivation is unreliable when mood dips
- Approval sensitivity is your core wound; practice acting before you feel validated

## MBTI: INTP-T — The Logician

**Core strengths:** novel perspectives, pattern recognition, intellectual curiosity, adaptability, independent thinking, truth-focused over comfort-focused

**Core weaknesses:** difficulty completing tasks, resistant to imposed structure, impractical tendencies, poor self-promotion, procrastination, easily distracted by new ideas before finishing old ones

### Relevance to Work
The INTP excels at analysis, systems thinking, and finding the non-obvious angle. Struggles with routine execution, bureaucracy, and selling herself. The "-T" (turbulent) adds self-doubt and sensitivity to external feedback.

### Practical Mitigations
- Use external structure (time blocking, accountability partners) to compensate for internal follow-through gaps
- Pair with complementary types for execution — don't try to be the implementer and the strategist simultaneously
- Practice visible output: INTPs under-communicate, which reads as low contribution even when the thinking is high quality
