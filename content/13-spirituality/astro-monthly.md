# Monthly Astro Outlook — March 2026

## Background Pressure (All Month)

**Progressed Sun conjunct natal Neptune — 0°18'.** The single most important progression happening now. Identity is merging with Neptune — dissolution, spirituality, dreams, confusion, transcendence. Everything about who you think you are is being dissolved and reformed. Career identity, self-concept, ambitions, the story you tell about yourself — Neptune is softening all of it. Expect fog. Expect moments of spiritual clarity followed by weeks of "what am I doing." Both are the progression working.

**Progressed Moon conjunct natal Moon — 0°36' — and natal Jupiter — 0°49'.** This is a progressed lunar return, happening on top of your most powerful natal conjunction. Progressed lunar returns happen every 27.5 years. Emotional system resetting to factory settings. Whatever emotional patterns accumulated that aren't truly yours are being shed. Pay attention to what you feel in March that feels *new* but also deeply familiar. That's the reset.

**Transiting Nodes square natal Pluto — 0°07'.** Essentially exact. Something fated is happening this month in the realm of relationships or power.

**Transit Saturn square natal Sun — exact March 15.** Authority pressure on identity. External structures feel restrictive. The world is asking "what have you built?" and the answer might feel inadequate.

**Transit Uranus opposition natal Chiron — 0°47'.** Sudden disruption to the wound. Something unexpected shakes up 6th house Chiron themes — health, daily routines, the body.

## Week-by-Week Arc

**Week 1 (March 1–7) — The Insight Window.** Mercury crosses Moon-Jupiter. Something clicks mentally about career direction. March 7 is the most pleasant day of the month — best day for anything requiring charm, taste, or aesthetic judgment.

**Week 2 (March 8–14) — The Pressure Peak.** Jupiter forces Mars to act (square, exact March 9). March 11: Sun illuminates Moon-Jupiter — career purpose, emotional certainty, "yes, THIS is what I'm supposed to be doing." March 13: Venus trine Pluto and Mars square Pluto simultaneously — magnetic and volatile.

**Week 3 (March 15–21) — The Saturn Gate.** March 15 is the heaviest single day. Identity under maximum pressure. March 18: mental intensity — Mercury square Pluto, Mars square Mercury, Venus conjunct Mars. Channel into writing, not interpersonal confrontation.

**Week 4 (March 22–31) — The Activation.** Mars crosses Moon-Jupiter on March 29 — the action point of the month. Whatever insight landed in week 1, whatever pressure built in weeks 2–3, Mars demands action. The fog from Neptune-Sun won't clear. Act anyway. Mars doesn't wait for clarity. It creates it through movement.
