**Spring 2026 prediction:** back in a city, own space, roots established. Keep your space even when traveling.

## Career

- Cards show going from one extreme to another — a meaningful career shift.
- May require additional learning or training.
- Likely to enter a role at a lower level but rise quickly within 6 months as people see the work.
- Not going into a dead-end or stagnant job — there's growth.
- Called to help people: education, teaching, contributing to the public. Even within business/climate finance, need a position that allows mentoring or teaching others.
- **Reader on climate finance:** "You're creating the balance. You've got a perfect place. You can still be in the business realm."
- Main block: wanting the high position at the beginning. Need to evaluate — is there room for growth? That's the question to ask, not "is the entry point glamorous?"

## Love & Relationships

- Old soul — may connect more easily with people older than you.
- If with someone younger, be careful not to mother them.
- Reader sees marriage, no separation. Mid-30s timing.
- Past pattern: staying in relationships you know aren't right out of loyalty and not wanting to hurt them. Three or four dates without connection = stop forcing it.
- Practice: communicate instead of shutting down. Don't accumulate resentments until you blow up over something minor.
- Before ending something: distinguish intentional harm vs. accidental. Intentional = no need to maintain. Unintentional = they deserve communication.
- Don't force friendship with people who hurt you. That's bad karma and inauthentic.
