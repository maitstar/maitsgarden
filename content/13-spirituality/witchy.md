# Witchy & Woo Practices

## Rituals

- Blow cinnamon out front door on 1st of month
- Thank the house you live in
- Say thank you aloud when good things happen
- Give the first portion of something good away — set it aside for the spirits

## Psychic Reading Notes

- Embrace stability, leave chaos behind
- Understand emotions
- Press pedal
- When things are offered, see what my original plan is
- Keep your goal and plan in mind

## Reflection Questions (Quests)

- What past illusions and feelings have come up for me?
- Is your giving and nurturing reciprocated, or is it only going one direction? Are you able to receive?
- Do you take on others' emotional burdens? Do you feel your value is tied to merging and fixing and caring for others?
- What karmic lessons did I learn from Saturn in Pisces?
- How do I stay digitally secure in the age of new AI?
- How can we feel continuous with our future selves?
- Mercury Retrograde in Pisces was a chance to practice refining your message and ensuring that your words and decisions truly align with your intentions.
- The theme of this month is about including your heart in your relationships and resolving whatever is standing in the way of intimacy.
- Put salt on your head before you leave the house to neutralise evil eye
- Tell my problems to the moon
- Imagine I'm a princess and do princess rituals
