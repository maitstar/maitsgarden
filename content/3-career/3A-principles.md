1. You are either an analyst or an operator. Figure out what you are
2. Having a brand name on your resume opens up a lot of roles
3. To be perceived as:
    - **Low-maintenance** (never creates chaos or confusion)
    - **High-reliability** (deadlines hit, quality exceeds expectations)
    - **Proactive clarity engine** (they never need to chase or interpret you)
    - **No ego, no drama** (adjusts seamlessly to feedback or shifting tasks)
4. Your reputation is not your resume; it's the professional "scent" you leave behind. It's built by the high-quality questions you ask, the concise thank-you notes you send, and the professional courtesy you show to schedulers and assistants.
