# Job Search Essentialism

## The Core Rules

**Say no to good opportunities.** The hardest nos are to roles that are good but not great. Practice: "I'm focused on [specific thing]." "Not right now, but I'll reach out if that changes."

**The 10x question.** "If I could only send 3 applications this month, which ones?" Do those three — but make them absurdly good. "If I could only have 2 networking conversations, who?" Then only do those two.

**Hell Yeah or No for people.** Stop responding to LinkedIn recruiters at firms you'd never join. Only engage with people directly connected to target companies or conversations that genuinely excite you.

**Schedule it or it doesn't exist.** Block: post-interview decompress, weekly planning session, daily application time, recovery after networking calls.

**Sunk cost trap.** Cancel: that networking coffee from 3 weeks ago, the half-finished application to a role that doesn't excite you, informational interviews with companies you're no longer interested in.

**Protect the asset.** Sleep, medication, family stuff determine whether you show up well. Meditation/writing/rest aren't optional — they're strategic.

**Routine eliminates decision fatigue.** Same outfit, same pre-call ritual, same post-interview debrief questions.

## What to Cut
- Applications to roles you'd take only out of desperation
- Relationships that aren't leading anywhere
- "I should apply here because it's climate-adjacent" trap
- Productive activities that are actually just procrastination (systems planning can wait until you have a job)

## OPT Timeline (Reference)
**Real deadline: ~May 1** (need signed offer with runway to file extension paperwork). STEM OPT extension = 3 years of runway — far more employer-friendly than 1 year.

Week 1: Email ISSO to confirm STEM OPT eligibility. Build list of 50 H-1B sponsor companies (MyVisaJobs.com, H1BGrader.com). Find specific hiring managers, not HR.

Week 2: Columbia alumni outreach first (25–40% response rate vs. cold). 10–15 alumni messages/day. Template: short, specific, direct. "I came across your profile while researching [Company] — your path from Columbia to [role] is exactly the trajectory I'm working toward."

Target categories: Finance/Banking (JPM, GS, MS, Citi, BlackRock), Big 4 (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC, Accenture), Tech (Amazon, Google, Microsoft).

## Breaking Into Strategy (2026)
The traditional path is dead — agency models broken, consulting contracting, AI automating junior work. Go where talent isn't: logistics, healthcare infrastructure, energy transition, insurance, local government.

**Create value without permission.** Find local businesses (10–50 employees). Offer: "Let me spend a day understanding your business. I'll give you three actionable recommendations. If useful, we talk." Do this 5 times = better education than most agency strategists get in a decade.

**Non-automatable skills to build:** research interviewing, facilitation, synthesis (finding non-obvious connections, not summarizing), writing for decision-makers, quantitative literacy, reading rooms.
