# Outreach Templates & Networking System

> All networking strategy, email templates, event protocols, and VC-specific relationship building in one place.

---

## Core Rules

- **Under 200 words** for cold outreach — always
- **You can email anyone.** Works when: you're competent at something valuable + you demonstrate it + you have a specific ask
- **Exchange numbers, not social media.** Nothing substantive comes from LinkedIn/Instagram at events.
- **Follow up with value** before you ask for anything
- **Specificity signals competence.** Vague = forgettable.

---

## Cold Email Formula

**Structure:** Why them (1 line) → Who you are (2-3 lines) → The ask (1 line) → Warmth (1 line)

```
Hi [Name],

I came across your work on [specific thing — article, project, talk, company]. [One sentence on why it resonated.]

I'm [2-line positioning — what you do and what you're working toward]. I'm currently exploring [relevant area to their work].

Would you be open to a 20-minute call sometime in the next few weeks? Happy to work around your schedule.

Thanks either way —
[Your name]
```

## Referral / Informational Template

```
Hi [Name],

Hope you're well — [one line of genuine human connection].

I'm currently [where you are in your search — specific]. I've been targeting [type of role/firm] and wanted to get your perspective on [specific question — not "any jobs?"].

Would you have 15–20 minutes for a quick call?

No pressure — happy to send questions over email if that's easier.

[Your name]
```

**Ask for advice, not jobs. Be specific. Act on what they say. Report back.**

## Networking Event Protocol

**Before:** Write a list — who do I want to meet, what do I want to learn, what do I want to offer.

**During:** Associate when people tell you where they work. Snowball the conversation — goal is to understand WHO this person is. Exchange numbers before leaving, not LinkedIn.

**When leaving:** *"Hey, it was great to meet you — we should grab coffee. Can I get your number?"*

**After (within 24 hrs):** *"Really enjoyed talking to you tonight about [specific thing]. Would love to grab coffee — let me know when works."*

---

## Coffee Chat Protocol

**Preparation:** 3 specific questions, research one genuinely interesting thing about their work, have your 30-second pitch ready.

**During:** You are there to learn, not pitch. At the end: *"Is there anyone else you think I should talk to?"*

**After:** Thank-you note within 24 hours referencing something specific. Follow up with a progress update.

---

## Maintaining the Network

**Monthly touchpoint rule.** Reach out to all mentors and recent connections once a month.

**Escalation path (contact → champion):**
1. Informational chat (advice, specific questions)
2. Act on advice, report back
3. Second conversation with update
4. Ask: *"Is there anyone at [company] you think I should speak with?"*
5. If they offer to refer, send materials immediately

End calls with: *"This was really helpful. Is there one other person you'd recommend I speak with given my focus areas?"*

---

## Networking in VC (Relationship-First)

**Quality over quantity.** Fewer, deeper relationships over collecting contacts. Aim for fellow travelers who share your values (Heidi Roizen).

**Create value first:** Thoughtful insights on their work, relevant introductions, research aligned with their thesis, meaningful comments on their writing.

**Be consistent and patient.** Quarterly check-in cadence. Track progress over months, not days.

**Communities:** Girls Into VC, VC Platform, On Deck VC, accelerator programs, venture scout programs.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Asking "any jobs?" on first contact
- Following up once then ghosting
- Connecting on LinkedIn without a message
- Generic emails with no specific hook
- Over-explaining your situation
- Not preparing questions before coffee chats
