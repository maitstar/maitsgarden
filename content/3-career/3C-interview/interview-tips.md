# Interview Masterclass

> Complete interview system — framework, question bank, delivery techniques, preparation protocol.

---

## Core Frame

**Interviews are designed to disqualify you.** Your job: survive every filter, then stand out.

**Act like a peer, never a subordinate.** You are evaluating them as much as they are evaluating you.

**Signpost as you go.** Say things that give the interviewer threads to pull — the natural follow-up question should always be the one you want to answer next.

**Say something interesting in the first 30 seconds.** If they're not leaning forward by then, you've lost them.

---

## Tell Me About Yourself — Hook + Bridge + Proof

- **Hook:** Lead with your unique market insight or perspective
- **Bridge:** Connect your experiences through a coherent narrative thread
- **Proof:** End with a concrete example that demonstrates your value proposition

Tailor the narrative for the specific role:
- VC interview → analyst/researcher identity, thesis-builder
- Climate org → values-aligned practitioner with analytical grounding
- Corp finance → polished specialist with quantitative edge
- Startup → scrappy generalist with high ownership

## Question-by-Question Protocols

**Greatest weakness:** Offer a genuine but non-critical flaw you've actively worked to improve. Formula: *"I used to [flaw]. I noticed it when [example]. Now I [what I do differently], and I've seen improvement in [evidence]."*

**Why this company/role:** Reference something specific — a report they published, a portfolio company, a thesis, a hire. End with a forward-looking connection between your trajectory and theirs.

**Tell me about a challenge (STAR):** Situation → Task → Action → Result. Quantify the result, even qualitatively. End with what you learned.

**Where do you see yourself in 5 years:** Be honest about ambition but frame it as growth within the org's ecosystem. Not *"I want to grow"* — say *"I want to own X type of work and move toward Y responsibility."*

**Do you have any questions for us:** Never say no. Minimum 3 questions ready:
- *"What does success look like in this role after 6 months?"*
- *"What's the biggest challenge the team is working on right now?"*
- *"How does the work here connect to the broader mission?"*

## Preparation Protocol

**Before:**
1. Read the job description 3 times — annotate for key competencies
2. Research the interviewer on LinkedIn (1 interesting thing to reference naturally)
3. Research the company: most recent news, fund thesis, major portfolio/projects
4. Prepare your 30-sec / 2-min / 5-min story versions
5. Prepare 3 STAR stories that can flex across multiple questions
6. Prepare 3 questions for them
7. Block a 30-min decompress slot immediately after

**Day of:** Arrive/join 5 minutes early. Phone on silent. Glass of water.

**After:** Thank-you note within 24 hours. Log what questions came up, what landed, what didn't.

## Behavioural Question Bank

**Story 1 — Leading through ambiguity / ownership**
Covers: *"Tell me about a time you dealt with uncertainty" / "Biggest challenge" / "Decision without full information"*

**Story 2 — Collaboration / stakeholder management**
Covers: *"Work with a difficult colleague" / "Cross-functional project" / "Influence without authority"*

**Story 3 — Impact / results under constraints**
Covers: *"Biggest achievement" / "When you did more with less" / "Project you're most proud of"*

**Story 4 — Self-awareness / growth**
Covers: *"Greatest weakness" / "What would colleagues say about you" / "What would you do differently"*

## Follow-Up Templates

**Post-interview (within 24 hrs):**
*"Hi [Name], thank you for your time today. I especially appreciated [something specific]. It reinforced my excitement about [specific aspect of the role/team]. I'd love to stay in touch regardless of how the process unfolds."*

**Post-rejection:**
*"Hi [Name], thank you for letting me know. I have a lot of respect for what [company] is building and would love to be considered for future opportunities."*

**Post-coffee chat:**
*"Hi [Name], I took note of what you said about [X]. Here's how it's coming along: [brief update]. Could I tell you more in person when you're available?"*

## At Work (Making Yourself Indispensable)

1. Come with options, not problems — bring A, B, C
2. Be the timeline anchor — volunteer to own the calendar
3. Pre-solve ambiguity — never ask "What should I do?" Frame it as a confirmation
4. Send drafts early, iterate often. Never go dark for days.
5. Name files cleanly: `ProjectName_Draft3_0612.pptx`
6. If late: say when you'll deliver AND in what form
7. Always clarify "done" upfront

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Vague, unquantified answers
- Generic company research (*"I love your mission"* without specifics)
- Scheduling anything immediately after an interview
- Not preparing questions for them
- Failing to follow up within 24 hours

## Quick Tips

- Don't wear glasses during interviews
- Coffee chat rule of 3: there should be 3 things for them to take away from you
- Tell me about yourself: 3 points — personal summary, accomplishments, forward-looking
- Financial modelling best practices — study before any finance role interview
