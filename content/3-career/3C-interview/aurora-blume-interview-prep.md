# Interview Prep — Aurora & Blume Ventures

## Aurora Energy Research — Commercial Associate

**Interviewer:** William Lewis, Head of Commercial APAC | Format: 30 min, Teams

### 90-Second Opener
"I've spent the last few years on the investment and commercial side of clean energy — evaluating deals, building partnerships, and co-founding a company. The common thread is that I'm always most energized when I'm talking to the people making decisions, not writing the memo that sits in a folder." Columbia MPA, David Leuschen Energy Fellow at CGEP → VC at Peak Sustainability and Regeneration VC → CleanCrop co-founder. Close: "That's the background. What drew me to Aurora specifically is that I've been the person who needs the intelligence Aurora provides. Now I want to be on the side that delivers it."

### Key Framing
- **Why commercial?** Outcome is unambiguous — either you close or you don't
- **Why not stay in VC?** Advisory is project-by-project; sales is where intelligence lands with real-time feedback
- **No quota experience?** "I haven't carried a formal quota, but I've had to sell without a product, without a brand, and without a CRM. Getting JALSA to partner on an agricultural trial in West Bengal with a two-person startup is harder than working a warm pipeline."
- **Why India/APAC?** Based in Kochi — not parachuting, already in the market

### Products
EOS platform: Origin (power scenarios), Chronos (storage valuation). India 2030 case: ~25 GW battery storage (top-down: 500 GW RE target, 15% storage need on 300 GW variable RE = 45 GW, conservative penetration = 25 GW).

### Lead Qualification (BANT)
Budget ($50K+ for EOS?), Authority (decision-maker?), Need (renewables forecasting?), Timeline. Score high: Indian developer like Adani needing storage models.

## Blume Ventures — Deeptech VC

### Anti-Ramble Rules
1. Answer in 3 sentences max. Then stop and wait.
2. If you feel yourself explaining context before the point, start over with the point.
3. Every answer ends with what *you* did, not what the team did.
4. If you don't know something: "I don't know yet, here's how I'd find out."

### 4 Core Stories

| Story | One-line anchor |
|---|---|
| Peanut allergy | Caught channel-blocking market risk week 1, no food science background |
| CCUS narrowing | Solo research shifted fund's sourcing to one subcategory: CO2-to-cement |
| Accelerator build | Built from scratch, now 50-person org, led sourcing + ops + IR |
| ASEAN grid | 17 interviews, zero prior background, findings adopted by ADB and IFC |

### Key Q&A
- **Bet in India deeptech?** Precision ag hardware for smallholders. AWD irrigation — 10 years underinvested, carbon credit market now willing to pay.
- **Why VC over operating?** I've operated. I want to see 50 of these problems and help allocate capital to the right one. Pattern recognition at scale.
- **Biggest weakness?** Rambling when excited. Working on: point first, context second.
- **Values they want:** Empathy (17 Gambia interviews), Humility ("I updated my CCUS view"), Long-term thinking, Craftsmanship (CCSI report, IC-ready memo), Work ethic (CRM of 3,500 contacts, co-founded org in grad school).
