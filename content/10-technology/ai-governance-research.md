# AI Governance Research Ideas (2024)

A collated list of 78 open research questions in AI governance by Markus Anderljung and Moritz von Knebel at GovAI. Covers corporate governance, regulation, compute, military applications, biosecurity, and market structure.

## Research Categories

**Activism & Advocacy**
- Public shareholder activism to shape AI trajectory
- Venture funding models as a governance lever

**AI & the Military**
- WMDs/resilience and risks from AI
- Likelihood and implications of AI nationalisation

**AI Market Structure**
- How concentrated will the AI market become?
- Business strategy behind releasing model weights (open vs. closed)
- Widespread use of open-access models
- Who will absorb the economic value produced by AI?

**Corporate Governance**
- Benchmarking investments into AI safety
- AI and whistleblowing protections
- Incident detection and monitoring at AI companies
- Publication norms in AI research

**Economic Growth / Explosive Growth**
- Comparing cost of computing to cost of human labour
- Forecasting AI impacts on workers, trade, developing countries
- Bottlenecks in chip manufacturing
- AI acceleration of foundation model and hardware development

**Regulation & Policy**
- Audit regimes for AI systems
- Liability frameworks for AI harms
- International coordination mechanisms

**Technical & Compute Governance**
- Compute thresholds as a regulatory trigger
- Hardware controls and export restrictions
- Evaluation standards for frontier models

**Bio × AI**
- Global pact on biosecurity controls at the digital-physical interface
- Policies to regulate biological design tools

## Why It's Relevant

AI governance intersects with climate finance, impact investing, and development — all areas where understanding regulatory trajectory matters for investment thesis. Markus Anderljung is a key figure worth following. See [[person-markus-anderljung]].
